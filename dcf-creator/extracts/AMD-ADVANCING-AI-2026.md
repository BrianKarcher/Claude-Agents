## TAM figures (Lisa Su, Advancing AI 2026 event)
| Market | Prior call | New 2030 call | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data center AI **accelerator** TAM | ~$500B by 2028 | **~$1.4T by 2030** | >45% CAGR |
| **Server CPU** TAM | ~$60B | **~$200–220B by 2030** | >50% CAGR |
| Combined HPC + AI compute TAM | — | **~$2T by 2030** | ~40% CAGR |

Reported phrasing: AMD "now sees the high performance and AI computing
market growing approximately 40% annually over the next several years,
approaching $2 trillion by 2030." Accelerator TAM "moved from last year's
$500 billion-by-2028 call to $1.4 trillion by 2030."

## How this is used in the DCF
- $1.4T accelerator TAM held **fixed across all 3 scenarios**; AMD's *share*
  is the variable (Bear 3.3% / Base 5.5% / Bull 8.8%).
- Server CPU TAM: Bull $220B, Base $165B, Bear $130B (only the AI-hardware
  TAM was instructed to be fixed).
- Warrant packages (Meta Feb 2026, OpenAI Oct 2025) referenced from separate
  reporting, not this event.

## Caveat
A $1.4T accelerator TAM in 2030 implies ~45%/yr compounding of AI-infra
spend for four more years with no digestion pause — no hardware cycle in
history has sustained that. The DCF adopts it as a stated premise, not an
endorsed forecast.
