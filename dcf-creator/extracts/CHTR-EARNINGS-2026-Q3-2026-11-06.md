## 7. NEW OPERATING MODEL, SYNERGIES, GUIDANCE

New operating model:
- Reformulated go-to-market for Internet, marketing and distribution, with mobile and video used to drive growth.
- Organizational redesign. Nick Jeffery (ex-Frontier CEO, ex-Vodafone UK) joined as COO on Sep 1, 2026 to lead it.

Savings:
- $2B incremental annualized run-rate opex savings over 18 months, a "bottoms-up plan".
  - Levers: cut projects that don't drive Internet growth, wider spans of control, fewer layers, fewer vendors.
  - "Not targeted at frontline service and sales employees."
  - The share to be REINVESTED is not quantified. Cost to achieve and severance are not quantified.
- Cox synergies: "more than $1 billion" run-rate opex, up from "at least $800M" in Q2 and $500M at signing. Nearly half
  lands in 1H'27; 90% within 24 months. Capex synergies are excluded.
- Legacy Cox customer service returns to the US within 18 months.

Guidance and outlook:
- 2026 PF capex ~$13.0B excluding transition; 2027 capex at or below $11B.
- Leverage below 3.9x at YE26; 3.5x by ~Aug 2029.
- 2026 cash tax $500-800M.
- No EBITDA guidance and no timeline for a return to growth: "We're not providing any guidance or outlook for exactly
  when we return to growth." Winfrey: "We will return Charter to Internet customer growth."
- NOT provided: 2027 EBITDA, 2027 cash taxes, combined run-rate capex, the reinvestment share, cost to achieve.

Restatements: none disclosed. The definitions are new (actual vs PF; legacy Cox counts are residential only, with SMB and
mid/large stats reflecting legacy Charter only).
