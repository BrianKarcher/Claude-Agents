## SEGMENT HISTORY — read first: two structures, and a bridge between them

| Period | Structure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FY2018–FY2024, as originally reported | **CNBU** (Compute & Networking: DRAM for data center, PC, graphics, networking), **MBU** (Mobile: DRAM + NAND for smartphones), **SBU** (Storage: all SSDs, data center + client + consumer, plus component NAND), **EBU** (Embedded: auto, industrial, consumer) | FY2020/22/24 10-Ks |
| FY2023 onward, recast (reorganization completed in FY2025) | **CMBU** (Cloud Memory: DRAM for large hyperscalers + ALL HBM), **CDBU** (Core Data Center: all data center storage/SSD + DRAM for OEM, enterprise and, from FY26, NeoCloud), **MCBU** (Mobile & Client: phones + PCs, DRAM and NAND), **AEBU** (Automotive & Embedded) | FY2025/26 10-Ks |

The CDBU definition wording changed between the FY2025 10-K ("mid-tier cloud, enterprise, and OEM data center") and the FY2026 10-K ("OEM data center, enterprise, and NeoCloud"). FY2024 and FY2025 CDBU figures are identical in both 10-Ks, so no restatement resulted.

## HBM / data-center mix disclosures (limited — Micron stopped giving an HBM dollar figure)

- **FQ4 FY25:** HBM revenue was "nearly $2B", about an $8B annualized run-rate. HBM, high-capacity DIMMs and LP server DRAM together were about $10B in FY25. Source: FQ4 FY25 call per secondary coverage (Futurum, Constellation Research); the primary transcript was not re-read.
- **FQ3 FY26:** HBM4 revenue had passed $1B. The 2026 HBM supply was sold out, and Micron could meet only about half to two-thirds of HBM demand. Source: TheNextWeb, Jun 24, 2026 (secondary).
- **HBM TAM:** about $100B by CY2028, per Micron's FQ1 FY26 call (Dec 2025), up from about $35B in 2025.
- **FQ4 FY26 prepared remarks (primary):**
  - HBM revenue "grew faster than total company revenue in the quarter."
  - Micron has "completed agreements for the vast majority of our calendar 2027 HBM bit supply with significant price increases year over year, narrowing the gross margin gap with conventional DRAM."
  - It is working on NVHBM, NVIDIA's custom HBM4E.
  - No HBM dollar figure is given.
- **Data-center SSD in FQ4 FY26:** "nearly $10 billion", more than 10x the year-ago quarter and over two-thirds of NAND revenue.
- **FY2026 overall:** "data center revenue up fourfold." DRAM revenue exceeded $100B.
- No customer was ≥10% of FY26 revenue. In FY25, one customer was 17% (primarily CMBU).
