## 1. Reportable segments (Tesla reports TWO: Automotive, Energy Generation & Storage)

Tesla's CODM evaluates segments on **gross profit only** — no segment operating income is
disclosed. "Automotive segment" = automotive sales + regulatory credits + leasing + **services
and other** (which is where Robotaxi ride-hail revenue, FSD subscriptions, Supercharging,
insurance, used cars, and service all sit). **Robotaxi has no separately disclosed revenue or
profit in any filing to date** — the Q2 2026 10-Q mentions it only in XBRL tags.
