## One-Time Items / Restatements

- **$4.5B charge in Q1 FY2026** for H20 excess inventory and related purchase obligations, tied to U.S. export controls restricting China sales of the H20 chip. This depressed Compute & Networking segment operating income for the full fiscal year and should be treated as a non-recurring item when trending FY2026 margins against prior/future years.
