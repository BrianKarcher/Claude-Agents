## FQ1 FY27 guidance and FY27 commentary
- **FQ1 FY27 guidance:**
  - Revenue $61.5B ± $1.5B.
  - GAAP GM ~85.95%; non-GAAP ~86.25%.
  - GAAP opex ~$2.31B; non-GAAP ~$2.06B.
  - GAAP EPS $37.84 ± $1.00; non-GAAP EPS $38.15 ± $1.00.
  - About 1.15B diluted shares.
- "We expect fiscal 2027 to be another record year, with sequential revenue growth each quarter."
- "We anticipate fiscal Q1 to be the floor for gross margins in fiscal 2027." Gross margins are expected to be higher after FQ1, "with a more moderate rate of price increases."
- FY27 opex is expected to rise ~$2.5B, from higher R&D and incentive comp.
- FY27 tax rate is expected at ~15.5%.
- **Capex (net of anticipated government incentives):**
  - ~$11.5B in FQ1 FY27 and ~$25B in H1 FY27.
  - "Higher in the second half of fiscal 2027."
  - Construction capex will grow meaningfully faster than equipment capex. The increase over prior plans is mostly construction, to accelerate cleanroom availability from late CY2028 on.
- **Industry outlook:**
  - Supply-demand is expected to be "much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028 than they were in 2026."
  - Industry DRAM bit shipments: mid-20s % growth in CY26 and low-20s % in CY27 and CY28, with the industry "supply constrained in both years."
  - Industry NAND bit shipments: low-20s % growth in CY26 and mid-20s % in CY27 and CY28.
  - Industry HBM bits are expected to grow faster than conventional DRAM through CY2028.
  - "we do not have line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance."
- Server units are expected to grow by a high-teens percentage in both CY26 and CY27.
- PC and mobile units may decline by double digits, but industry revenue grows on premium mix.

## Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs) — the structural-change argument
- 26 SCAs have been signed, estimated at "over 35% of our revenue through 2030." Some extend into 2031.
- Three-quarters of the estimated SCA revenue has a defined pricing framework, mostly bands with floors and ceilings. The remaining quarter is priced periodically at market.
- All SCAs are take-or-pay on volume. Customer financial commitments total $32B, "the vast majority" of it cash deposits.
- The deposits are unrestricted. They are returned toward the latter half of each term if minimum purchases are met.
- **RPO:** ~$150B per the prepared remarks; the 10-K figure is ~$134B. The 10-K figure:
  - is based on committed volumes × MINIMUM prices;
  - includes $13B already recognized as contract liabilities;
  - is about one-quarter recognized in the next 12 months and fully recognized within 5 years.
- "even at floor prices, we expect margins meaningfully above any prior cycle peak margins." (CFO, FQ4 FY26). Prior peak: FY2018 GM 58.9% and op margin 49.3%.
- Customers maintain $7B of letters of credit, accessible to Micron only on default or breach.

## Government incentives / CHIPS Act (10-K Note 15)
- Incentives have reduced PP&E by a cumulative $10.93B as of Sep 3, 2026. $6.43B of that relates to FY2026 expenditures, against cash proceeds of $3.316B in FY26. The remainder sits in receivables and other noncurrent assets: $3,319M and $2,122M respectively.
- FY26 operating income benefited by $901M from incentives that reduced depreciation and other expenses.
- **CHIPS direct funding: up to $6.4B.**
  - $6.1B is for Idaho (ID1 + ID2) and New York; $275M is for Manassas.
  - The remaining unrecognized commitment is $4.9B as of Sep 3, 2026.
  - Disbursement is milestone-based: construction, tool install, wafer output.
- **Clawback and upside-sharing:**
  - Clawback applies for failing to complete a project by the agreed date, for foreign-entity-of-concern violations, and for impermissible use.
  - Upside sharing runs for up to 10 years after the first year a project's cumulative cash flow is positive, if that cash flow is "at a significant premium" to baseline. It is capped at 75% of disbursements.
- **Capital-return restrictions:**
  - Special dividends are restricted for 5 years from Dec 9, 2024.
  - Buybacks were limited for the first 2 years. For years 3–5 (from Dec 9, 2026), they are subject to FCF-based limits, net of CHIPS capex grants and dividends.
  - Micron says it will "increase our capital return from Dec. 9, 2026." The board raised the authorization to $35.16B effective Dec 9, 2026. Over time, Micron intends to return "100% of our excess cash."
- **Section 48D investment tax credit:**
  - Raised from 25% to **35%** by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act for property placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.
  - **The credit applies only to property whose construction BEGINS before Jan 1, 2027** (statute; the deadline was not extended by OBBBA, per BDO/PwC summaries).
  - So ID1, ID2 and NY fab 1 should qualify. NY fabs 2–4 likely will not unless construction formally begins by Dec 31, 2026. This is an inference, not a Micron statement.
  - The credit is recognized as a receivable/other asset, reduces the PP&E basis, and is monetizable through direct pay.
- **Other incentives:**
  - New York State: a non-binding term sheet for up to $5.5B over 20+ years.
  - Japan (METI): ¥536B (~$3.4B) remaining unrecognized.
  - India: ₹102B (~$1.1B) remaining, covering 50% central + 20% state of the Gujarat project cost.
  - Singapore: arrangements finalized Nov 2025 and Apr 2026, terms confidential.
