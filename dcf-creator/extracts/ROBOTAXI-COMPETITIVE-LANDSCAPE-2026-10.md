## TAM estimates (wide range — definitions differ)

| Source | Figure | Scope / note |
|---|---|---|

Existing ride-hail spend as a reality anchor:
- **Uber Mobility gross bookings Q2 2026: $28.99B** (+20% cc) → ~$116B annualized. Total Uber gross
  bookings $58.0B (+24%); 3.9B trips (+18%); revenue $14.2B; GAAP operating income $1.9B.
- Uber FY2025 total gross bookings ~$193B (aggregator; unconfirmed vs 10-K).
- Lyft FY2025 gross bookings $18.5B (+15%).

## Competitors

**Waymo (Alphabet)**
- $16B raise closed Feb 2, 2026 at **$126B post-money** (led by Dragoneer, DST, Sequoia; Alphabet ~$13B).
  Prior: $5.6B at $45B (Oct 2024).
- Annualized revenue run-rate >$350M (single source, unverified).
- **500k+ paid rides/week** (held since Mar 2026); goal >1M/week by YE2026.
- Fleet: "over 3,000" (Waymo's own) / 3,871 (June 2026 recall filing) / 4,000+ (Sept 2026 report).
- **14 US cities** after Denver, San Diego, Tampa launched Sept 1, 2026. Some Austin/Atlanta rides via
  the Uber app.
- Owns its software stack; buys vehicles (Jaguar I-Pace, Zeekr/Hyundai) from third parties. Lidar +
  radar + camera.

**Uber**
- AV aggregator strategy: partnered with/invested in **30+ AV companies** (TechCrunch, Aug 2026;
  older counts 20-25+). Named: Waymo, Nuro, Lucid (≥20,000 Lucid Gravity robotaxis w/ Nuro stack from
  2026), Baidu & WeRide (Middle East), Momenta & Wayve (Europe).
- Targets: AV rides in 15 cities by YE2026; "largest facilitator of AV trips" by 2029. ~$10B AV commitment reported.

**China (largely closed to Tesla in the fleet model)**
- Baidu Apollo Go: 20M+ cumulative rides (Feb 2026; 22M by Apr per another source), ~27 cities,
  per-vehicle profitable in Wuhan since late 2025 (per Baidu).
- Pony.ai: 1,975 robotaxis (June 30, 2026), target 3,500+ by YE2026.
- WeRide: 1,800+ robotaxis within ~3,400 L4 fleet (July 31, 2026).

**Zoox (Amazon)**: purpose-built, bidirectional vehicle; public rides in Las Vegas still FREE
(paid-service exemption pending); ~500k riders (June 2026); testing SF, Austin, Miami.

## Pricing comparison

Obi study, San Francisco, late Nov 2025 – Jan 1 2026 (Tesla there had safety drivers; likely subsidized):

| | Avg fare | Median fare |
|---|--:|--:|
| Tesla | $8.17 | $7.39 |
| Lyft | $15.47 | $12.99 |
| Uber | $17.47 | $14.94 |
| Waymo | $19.69 | $17.25 |

- Tesla ≈ $1.99/km — about a third of the others. Waymo's premium over Uber narrowed from 30–40%
  (June 2025) to ~12.7% (late 2025). Waymo avg pickup 5.74 min vs Uber 3.15.
- Tesla raised Austin pricing Mar 7, 2026: $3.00 base + $1.40/mi (was $1.00/mi).
