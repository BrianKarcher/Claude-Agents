## Headline numbers ($M unless noted)
- Revenue: $890.4, +49% YoY (US $657.1, +21%; international/"Rest of World" $233.3, +321%). About $135M of international came from Eucalyptus. The rest of international grew ~80% (call).
- $18M of weight-loss membership revenue was deferred into Q4 (call). It is not in the $890.4.
- Subscribers: 2,788K, +13% YoY but **down ~100K QoQ** from ~2.89M in Q2. The 2.89M figure comes from technews.tw, not a primary source. The consensus cited there was ~3.04M. Management attributed the drop to paced domestic marketing and international brand migrations, and warned of another quarter of subscriber headwinds.
- Monthly revenue per average subscriber: $105, +30% YoY ($81 in Q3'25).
- Gross margin: 57.2% (GP $509.2), vs 74% in Q3'25 and 64% in Q2'26. Drivers were the full-quarter Eucalyptus consolidation and branded-GLP-1 mix.
- Opex as % of revenue: marketing 27.8% (down ~7 pts QoQ, deliberately paced), ops & support 12.0%, tech & dev 7.8%, G&A 17.0%.
- GAAP operating loss: $(66.1). Net loss: $(33.0), EPS $(0.14). Consensus was about +$0.06 (secondary).
- Adj. EBITDA: $82.4 (9.3% margin), inside the $75–95 guide but below its midpoint. **Definition change:** Q3 is the first quarter to add back FX gains/losses ($4.1M). On the old definition Q3 would have been ~$78.3. Prior periods were not recast.
- SBC: $64.5 (7.2% of revenue; was $40.2 in Q3'25). D&A: $38.2 ($10.9 PP&E/software + $27.3 intangibles).
- Excluded from Adj. EBITDA in Q3: acquisition/transaction/earn-out costs $35.4, restructuring $3.8, other non-recurring $5.6, FX $4.1, payroll tax on SBC $1.3.
- Operating cash flow: $65.4. CapEx $39.4 ($33.3 PP&E/intangibles + $6.1 website/internal-use software). FCF: $26.0.
- **FCF quality caveat:** In Q3 HIMS sold $434.5M of receivables under a new JPMorgan Receivables Purchase Agreement, for $433.3M of net proceeds. The facility is uncommitted, with a $400M limit outstanding at any time. Transfers are accounted for as sales and the proceeds are reported in operating cash flow. The Q3 OCF benefit equals the sold-but-uncollected balance at 9/30, which is not disclosed but is capped at $400M. This is effectively off-balance-sheet working-capital financing of the new manufacturer rebate receivables ($345.3M at 9/30, from the Novo/Lilly supply agreements).

## Guidance (11-09-2026)
- Q4 2026: revenue $875–895M (+42–45% YoY); adj. EBITDA $70–90M (8–10%).
- FY2026: revenue **$3.13–3.15B** (tightened from $3.1–3.3B; midpoint down from $3.20B to $3.14B). Adj. EBITDA **$257–277M** (cut from $275–325M, the third cut of 2026 after $275–350M at Q1). Management attributed the cut to UK/Germany pricing and marketing investment plus the membership-revenue deferral (≥$20M hit to FY revenue and EBITDA as December payments are recognized in January).
- FY2026 international revenue: ≥$650M (raised from $600M+). International expected to exceed a $1B run-rate in 2027.
- FCF: positive for FY2026 (9M = $10.8M), and positioned for positive FCF again in 2027.
- **2030 targets ($6.5B+ revenue / $1.3B adj. EBITDA) were not mentioned** in the release, and I did not find them in the partial transcript. Management said detailed long-term metrics and the 2027 outlook would come next quarter. They have been neither reaffirmed nor withdrawn.

## Strategic themes (call)
1. **Weight loss / partners.** Novo Nordisk: Wegovy pen and pill are on the platform, and CagriSema will be added once available. Eli Lilly: a new agreement (announced 11-06-2026) adds Zepbound and oral Foundayo. Self-pay pricing is reportedly from $149/mo (Foundayo) and $299/mo (Zepbound), and HIMS reportedly agreed to stop offering compounded copies of Lilly drugs (247wallst/technews, secondary). Multi-month subscriptions are planned before the new year. Canada generics lifted average monthly weight-loss orders >50%.
   - *Note on "Novo talks":* the widely circulated "HIMS in active talks with Novo Nordisk" headlines date from the **Q3 2025** call (11-03-2025), not Q3 2026. In Q3 2026 the Novo relationship is operating. Novo's Feb-2026 patent suit was dismissed without prejudice on 3/9/2026, so Novo could refile (10-Q).
2. **Marketing deliberately paced** ahead of the Q1 seasonal peak. Subscriber growth is expected to recover from Q1 2027.
3. **International.** Hers is live in the UK and Australia, and Hims in Germany and Australia. Aggressive pricing and marketing are planned in the UK/Germany. Brand consolidation will cause near-term subscriber volatility. Profitability by geography is not disclosed.
4. **AI.** The AI-native experience reached ~70% of weight-loss subscribers at quarter end and is expected to reach nearly all of them by year end, with ~4x message volume. Q3 was the largest engineering-hiring quarter in company history, which ties to the SBC jump.
5. **Hormones / peptides.** Injectable and oral TRT have launched (HIMS's first controlled substance). Testosterone is targeting a $100M annualized run-rate. Hers is approaching ~$1B revenue in 2026. "Peptides Plus" phase 1 (Sermorelin, glutathione, NAD+) launches late November. BPC-157 and TB-500 await regulators: an HHS proposal is in comment, and management expects FDA rulemaking "within weeks". Peptide revenue will not be material in Q4. Andrew Huberman joined the board (11-11-2026).

## Legal / regulatory
- **FTC** (10-Q): complaint filed 7/29/2026 in N.D. Cal. by the FTC, Utah, and LA County, seeking an injunction, monetary relief, and civil penalties. HIMS moved to dismiss on 10/22/2026, with the hearing **set for 1/14/2027** (a Stocktwits article says 1/7; the 10-Q governs). HIMS has accrued ~$60M under ASC 450 and says the accrual "may decrease or increase materially". It cannot estimate a reasonably possible loss above the accrual. **Amended complaint, 11/10/2026** (MLex/Stocktwits, not in the 10-Q because it was filed the day after): adds AZ, FL, NE, OH, and WI as co-plaintiffs, and alleges health-data sharing with ad platforms plus billing and cancellation practices. HIMS calls the claims "baseless".
- Also pending: a related *Doe* privacy class action, two securities class actions (*Velanki*, filed 9/1/2026; *In re HIMS Securities Litigation*, filed 6/2025), stayed derivative suits, and Australian TGA notices to Eucalyptus subsidiaries (indemnified by the warrantors).

## Segments

International profitability is qualitative only: "near breakeven" at Q2, and now being invested into.
