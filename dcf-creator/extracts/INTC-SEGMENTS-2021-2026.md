## Segment-definition history (READ FIRST — the series is NOT one continuous basis)

| Period | Structure | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| FY2021–FY2023 as originally reported | CCG, DCAI, NEX, AXG, Mobileye, IFS, Other | Pre-"internal foundry model." Not used here. |
| **Basis B (2024 structure)**: FY2021–FY2023 recast in the Apr 2, 2024 8-K | Intel Products = CCG + DCAI + NEX; Intel Foundry; All Other = Altera + Mobileye + Other | Created the internal foundry P&L. Intel Foundry now "sells" wafers to Intel Products at transfer prices. |
| **Basis A (2025 structure)**: FY2023–FY2025 in the FY2025 10-K and all 2025–2026 quarters | Intel Products = CCG + DCAI; Intel Foundry; All Other (Mobileye, IMS, startups, Altera through Sept 11, 2025) | Q1 2025: NEX folded into CCG and DCAI. **Corporate unallocated detail, 2026:**
- **Q1'26: $5,080M.** Restructuring and other $4,070M, which includes a **$3.9B non-cash goodwill impairment, substantially all Mobileye**. The rest is SBC $621M, acquisition-related $113M and other $276M. **Q1'26's GAAP operating loss was therefore driven by the Mobileye impairment, not by the segments: segment op income summed to +$1,723M that quarter.**
- **Q2'26: $1,416M.** Restructuring $170M, SBC $687M, acquisition $117M, other $442M.
- **H1'26 excluding restructuring:** $2,256M, about $4.5B annualized.

**Foundry external revenue, 2026:**
- Q1'26: $174M (Q1'25: $31M).
- Q2'26: $293M (Q2'25: $22M).
- H1'26: $467M. Of this, Altera, the former subsidiary that became an external customer after deconsolidation, accounted for **$181M in Q2'26 and $320M in H1'26**. **Non-Altera external foundry revenue was therefore only ~$112M in Q2'26 and ~$147M in H1'26.**
- Intersegment (internal) Foundry revenue was $5.5B in Q2'26 and $10.7B in H1'26 (~$21B/yr run-rate). Growth came from 18A/Intel 3/Intel 4 wafer volumes at higher ASPs.

The 10-Q attributes the Q1–Q2'26 Foundry profit pressure to an "increased mix of higher-cost wafers manufactured on our Intel 18A process node." The Q2'26 loss improvement vs Q2'25 was mainly the absence of $797M of impairment and accelerated-depreciation charges.

## Mobileye ownership (correction to earlier extracts)

Per the Mobileye Q2 2026 10-Q, Intel holds **all 597,768,015 Class B shares plus 50,000,000 Class A shares**, which is **~77.1% of Mobileye's outstanding common stock and 96.9% of voting power** as of Jun 27, 2026. Total outstanding: 242.2M Class A + 597.8M Class B = 840.0M. Intel's 10-Qs show Mobileye non-controlling interest of 23% (Mar and Jun 2026).

**Intel's stake is ~647.8M shares, ~$4.9B at $7.61/share (Sept 23, 2026 close), NOT ~50M shares / ~$0.4–0.5B.** The "~50M shares" figure in earlier sources/extracts counts only Intel's Class A holding and ignores its Class B shares.

## Key takeaways for modeling

1. Intel Products margin: FY23 22.9%, FY24 26.3%, FY25 25.9%, H1'26 31.8%. The 2026 step-up is entirely DCAI: 35.5% in H1'26 and 39.5% in Q2'26, vs 5.9–20.2% in FY23–25. CCG margin is flat-to-down (29.3% H1'26, 26.4% Q2'26, vs 34.8% in FY24) as 18A-based Panther Lake wafer costs flow through.
2. Intel Foundry loses ~$9–10B/yr excluding impairments (FY24 ~$10.0B, FY25 ~$9.4B, H1'26 annualized ~$9.1B). The trend is improving, but slowly, against management's 2027 breakeven target.
3. Corporate unallocated excluding restructuring runs ~SBC + $1–2B/yr.
4. DCAI 2026 is a recovery, not a new high, on the comparable Basis B. DCAI was $20.8B in FY2021 excluding NEX. FY2026E Basis-A DCAI of ~$24.8B includes former NEX data-center networking, so the like-for-like increase over 2021 is modest.
