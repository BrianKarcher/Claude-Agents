Intel's own reported GAAP operating income figures — which are what flow through to net income and match the official press releases — are the ones used in the table above and in the DCF. FY2021–FY2023 figures did not show this discrepancy (no large impairments those years) and were corroborated by direct 10-K citations found via search.

## The FY2024 collapse, explained

Q3 2024 alone carried **$15.9B of impairment charges** + **$2.8B of restructuring charges**:
- $3.1B non-cash impairment / accelerated depreciation on manufacturing assets (substantially Intel 7 node)
- ~$10.0B deferred-tax-asset impairment (cumulative GAAP losses triggered a valuation allowance)
- $2.6B goodwill impairment related to Mobileye
- $2.2B severance related to a ~15% headcount reduction
Total FY2024 restructuring-plan charges guided at ~$3.0B, with $2.8B cumulative recognized by Dec 28, 2024.

## Diluted share count context (dilution events, 2025–2026)

- Aug 23, 2025: US government agreement for a **9.9% stake** = 433.3M shares (~$8.9B value at announcement), funded by $5.7B of already-committed but unpaid CHIPS Act grants + $3.2B from the Secure Enclave program (on top of $2.2B already received via CHIPS grants — $11.1B total government support). Passive stake, no board seat / governance rights.
- SoftBank: **$2B investment** (~2% of shares) announced ~Aug 2025.
- Nvidia: agreement Sept 2025, private placement completed Dec 2025, **$5.0B** raised.
- Combined, these three events explain essentially all of the +664M-share increase in common shares outstanding from Dec 2024 (4,330M) to Dec 2025 (4,994M).
