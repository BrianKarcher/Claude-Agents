## Management guidance and commentary (Q2'26 call, July 24, 2026; quotes via Investing.com transcript)
- **2026 EBITDA:** "we expect standalone Charter EBITDA, excluding the impact of transition costs, to
  decline around 1% year-over-year." Previously "slight growth". This was a guidance CUT. H2 helps:
  political advertising, cost pass-throughs, efficiency.
- **2026 capex:** "approximately $11.4 billion" standalone.
- **Post-buildout capex:** "run rate capital expenditures for standalone Charter would be below $8
  billion per year" after network evolution and expansion conclude. The CFO referenced 2028. The
  reduction is worth "over $30 of FCF per share".
- **Cash taxes 2026:** $500-800M.
- **Leverage:** "lowering our post-transaction leverage target to a flat 3.5 times ... within three
  years of the close"; "not aspirational". Expected "just above 3.9 times" at end of Q3, assuming the
  Cox debt exchange offer succeeds.
- **Buybacks:** "paused ... through the end of the third quarter"; "expect share repurchases to restart
  in the fourth quarter". Buybacks to continue while deleveraging to 3.5x.
- **Cox synergies:** "at least $800 million per year" run-rate; "I think it'll grow to $1 billion."
  Implied Cox EV ~$27B, ~5x transaction EBITDA, ~4.4x with synergies.
- **Cox trends:** "a couple clicks lower than here at Spectrum" on subscribers and revenue.
- **Competition:** "expanded fixed-wireless competition versus a year ago"; "fiber overlap growth at a
  rate similar to prior quarters"; no "meaningful share loss to Starlink". Q2 churn "essentially flat";
  softer gross adds. "Internet customer growth is taking longer to reverse."
- **ARPU:** "Broadband ARPU will improve sequentially in Q3" as retention offers normalize and a cost
  pass-through takes effect.
- **Mobile:** 1.7M LTM line adds (+16%). Mobile+Internet customers churn "nearly 40% less". Wi-Fi/CBRS
  offload ~87%.
