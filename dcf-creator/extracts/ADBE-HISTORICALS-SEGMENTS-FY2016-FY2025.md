**Nine-year CAGRs (FY2016 → FY2025):** revenue **16.8%** · operating income **21.6%** · net income **22.3%** · diluted EPS **24.5%** · FCF **19.4%** · FCF per share **21.6%**. Diluted share count fell **15.3%** over the period (504M → 427M), so EPS and FCF per share outgrew their dollar totals. **FY2025 buybacks accelerated sharply** (−5.0% shares in one year vs. a ~1–2.5%/yr pace before).

**RPO CAGR FY2019 → FY2025:** **14.8%**, faster than revenue (~13.4% over the same span). RPO has grown to **0.95x** annual revenue from 0.88x.

§ **RPO is only disclosed from FY2019**, when Adobe adopted the ASC 606 revenue standard. For earlier years the closest proxy is deferred revenue (current + long-term) plus "unbilled deferred revenue backlog." On that basis it was ~**$6.43B** at FY2017 and ~**$8.10B** at FY2018 (deferred revenue $2,495M / $3,054M + unbilled backlog $3.94B / $5.05B, per the FY2018 10-K). It is **not comparable** to RPO and shouldn't be spliced into the series.

¶ "% next 12 months" excludes non-cancellable enterprise committed funds (~4–8% of RPO). The share has been **sliding steadily (74% → 65%)**, so contracts are getting longer. That improves visibility, but more of the backlog sits further out.

**FCF definition:** operating cash flow minus purchases of property & equipment, as reported. It is **before stock-based compensation** (SBC is added back in OCF). Capex has collapsed to **<1% of revenue** in FY2024–FY2025 (from ~2–3.5%), which flatters recent FCF margins. FY2024 FCF ex-Figma fee ≈ **$8,873M (41.3% margin)**.

† **FY2024 includes the $1.0B Figma termination fee** (deal abandoned Dec 2023), booked in operating expenses and paid in cash. Excluding it, FY2024 operating income is ~$7,741M (**~36.0%** margin), in line with FY2021/FY2025. Some aggregators (e.g., stockanalysis.com) show FY2024 operating income as $7,819M; the 10-K figure is $6,741M. Use the 10-K figure.

‡ **FY2020 net income is inflated by one-time tax benefits** of **$224M + $1.13B** from intra-entity transfers of IP rights (April and November 2020). Excluding them, FY2020 net income is ~$3.9B (~30% margin).

Share counts: FY2016–FY2019 from the FY2020 10-K's five-year selected data (FY2019 at 491.6M) and the FY2018 10-K; FY2020–FY2025 from the FY2022 and FY2025 10-K income statements. RPO from the revenue-recognition notes of each year's 10-K (FY2019–FY2025).

### Gross margin trend in one line
Gross margin was **85–87%** from FY2016 to FY2020. Since then it has climbed steadily to **89.3%**, driven by Digital Experience margins improving from ~62% to ~72% (see Section 3). Digital Media has been flat at ~95–96% the entire decade.

## 3. Segment results

Adobe reported three segments for the whole decade, but **their boundaries moved twice**, so the history comes in two blocks:
- **FY2018 reclass (in the FY2018 10-K):** legacy enterprise products (Adobe Connect, LiveCycle) moved from Digital Experience → Publishing. FY2016–FY2017 were restated, so Block A is internally consistent.
- **FY2020 reclass (in the FY2020 10-K):** **Advertising Cloud** moved from Digital Experience → Publishing, renamed **"Publishing and Advertising."** FY2018–FY2019 were restated, so Block B is internally consistent.
- **FY2018 appears in both blocks**, which shows the size of the move: Advertising Cloud ≈ **$371M** of FY2018 revenue (old DX $2,444M vs. new DX $2,073M).
- **FY2026 change:** starting Q1 FY2026, Adobe **combined all three into a single reportable segment**. **FY2025 is the last year of segment-level data.** Going forward, the closest substitutes are subscription revenue by customer group and Digital Media ARR.

Adobe's segment profit measure is **gross profit**. Operating expenses are not allocated to segments, so there is no segment operating income.

## 4. Key takeaways for valuation work

1. **Growth has stepped down twice:** ~24% (FY2017–FY2019) → ~15–23% (FY2020–FY2021, with COVID and a 53-week FY2021) → a steady **~10–11%** for four straight years (FY2022–FY2025).
2. **Margins expanded even as growth slowed.** Gross margin went from 86% to 89%. Operating margin went from 25.5% to 36.6%, flat at ~34–37% since FY2021 once the Figma fee is excluded.
3. **Digital Media is the whole company's profit engine:** 74% of revenue and **79% of gross profit** in FY2025 ($16.8B of $21.2B), at a ~95% gross margin.
4. **Digital Experience is maturing:** growth fell from 35% to 9%, but gross margin rose 10 points since FY2019.
5. **Publishing & Advertising is a runoff business:** about 1% of revenue, shrinking 7–24% a year since FY2020.
6. **There's no segment visibility from FY2026 on.** The AI/clone-disruption debate centers on Digital Media (Creative Cloud), and that's exactly the segment Adobe stopped reporting separately.
