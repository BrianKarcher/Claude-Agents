## 0. SEGMENT DISCLOSURE STATUS (READ FIRST)

- Charter has **ONE reportable segment** (Q2 2026 10-Q: "the Company has one reportable segment"; the
  segment measure is net income attributable to Charter shareholders).
- There is **no segment operating income**. Charter does not allocate costs or EBITDA to residential,
  SMB, enterprise, mobile or advertising.
- What Charter DOES disclose every quarter, and what this file uses as the segment-equivalent history:
  (a) revenue by product line (residential Internet/video/voice/mobile service, SMB, mid-market &
  large business, advertising, other); (b) customer and line counts and net adds; (c) capex by NCTA
  category.
- **Cox Communications** (closed 2026-08-19) is presented below as a separate acquired component, using
  the Cox historicals from Charter's own pro forma filings. Charter's Q3 2026 results, the first to
  include Cox (from Aug 19), are due 2026-11-06. **They were NOT available at the time of this pull.**
