WHAT THIS FILE IS (READ FIRST)
------------------------------
The main DCF builds consolidated revenue from EXTERNAL pieces only:

  Consolidated = Intel Products + All Other + Intel Foundry EXTERNAL

Intel's own segment note (ASC 280) presents the same total differently.
Each segment reports its revenue GROSS, including sales to other segments.
A separate "intersegment eliminations" line then removes those sales:

  Consolidated = Intel Products + Intel Foundry (internal + external)
                 + All Other + intersegment eliminations

This file restates the history AND all three forecast scenarios on Intel's
basis, so FY2021-H1'26 actuals and FY2026E-2036E projections can be read
row for row in the same layout.

WHAT IS IDENTICAL TO THE MAIN FILE (checked to the $1M, Section F):
  - Consolidated revenue, every scenario, every year.
  - Consolidated GAAP operating income, every scenario, every year.
  - CCG, DCAI, Intel Products, All Other and Foundry EXTERNAL revenue.
  - Every segment operating-income line (Section 4E of the main file).
  - Net capex, D&A and SBC, including the FY2026E capex projection
    (Section G1).
  - Therefore NOPAT, FCF, terminal values and every intrinsic value:
    Bull $38.71 / Base $12.79 / Bear ~$0 (-$0.54) / probability-weighted
    $14.60 / simple average $16.99.

WHAT IS NEW: the Foundry INTERNAL revenue line, the gross Foundry segment
revenue it produces, and the revenue elimination line that removes it.
Internal and elimination cancel exactly, so they change no total.

SECTION A — PRESENTATION RULES
================================================================================

THE BRIDGE BETWEEN THE TWO BASES
  Intel basis:
    Products + (Foundry internal + Foundry external) + All Other
      + elims, where elims = -(Foundry internal) + other intersegment
  = Products + Foundry external + All Other + other intersegment
  = main-file basis + other intersegment

  So the two presentations agree exactly whenever "other intersegment"
  is zero. That is the forecast assumption (rule 4 below), and it is the
  assumption the main file already makes implicitly.

FORECAST RULES (FY2026E-2036E)
  1. CCG, DCAI, Intel Products, All Other and Foundry EXTERNAL revenue are
     copied unchanged from main-file Section 4D.
  2. FOUNDRY INTERNAL REVENUE (sales to Intel Products) = 36.0% of Intel
     Products revenue in 2027E-2036E. This is the same ratio as the main
     file's Section 4D memo row. History on the current basis: 37.2%
     (FY2023), 34.7% (FY2024), 35.6% (FY2025), 38.4% (H1'26).
     FY2026E = H1'26 actual ($10,719M) + H2'26E at 38.0% of H2 Products
     ($30,382M x 38.0% = $11,545M) = $22,264M, matching main-file
     Section 4E's FY2026E Foundry build. This file carries internal revenue
     unrounded, to the $1M. The main file's memo row rounds Foundry segment
     revenue to $100M, so the two differ by at most $50M in any year. The
     Foundry margins below match main-file Section 4E exactly.
  3. INTEL FOUNDRY SEGMENT REVENUE = internal + external.
  4. INTERSEGMENT ELIMINATIONS (REVENUE) = -(Foundry internal) + other
     intersegment. "Other intersegment" is the small remainder Intel also
     eliminates, chiefly sales between Products / All Other and other
     segments: (67), (130), (164) in FY2023-25, (9) in H1'26. It is set to
     ZERO in every forecast year, as in the main file. Equivalently, the
     forecast Products and All Other lines are treated as net of their own
     intersegment sales. FY2026E follows main-file Section 4A: H1'26 actual
     (9), reversed in H2 (+9), so FY2026E is 0.
  5. OPERATING INCOME is copied unchanged from main-file Section 4E. That
     table is already on Intel's basis: segment OI, corporate unallocated
     and an intersegment-elimination line. The OI elimination is +$975M in
     FY2026E and 0 from 2027E. That judgment is unchanged here; see
     Section E.

WHY THE 36% RATIO DOES NOT AFFECT VALUE
  The internal ratio is a transfer-pricing assumption. Raising it moves
  revenue (and, at a given Foundry OI, margin) into the Foundry segment
  and the same amount into the elimination line. Consolidated revenue,
  consolidated OI and FCF do not move. It matters only for reading
  Foundry's segment revenue and segment margin against history.

SECTION E — WHAT THE INTEL-BASIS VIEW SHOWS
================================================================================
  - ELIMINATIONS ARE A QUARTER OF SEGMENT REVENUE, AND STAY THAT WAY
    EXCEPT IN BULL. Historically 24.6-26.5% of segment revenue total
    (FY2023-H1'26). The forecast holds 23-25% in Base and Bear. Only in
    Bull does it fall, to 19.4% by 2034-36, because external Foundry
    revenue grows into the segment. In Bull, external exceeds internal
    from 2032 (51.9% of the segment), the crossover main-file Section 4D
    describes.
  - FY2026E FOUNDRY SEGMENT REVENUE +30.5% IS ALMOST ALL INTERNAL. Of the
    +$5.4B, +$4.7B is internal (higher-ASP 18A / Intel 3 wafers sold to
    Products) and +$0.7B is external. None of the internal increase reaches
    consolidated revenue. This is the double-count main-file Section 3
    warns about, visible in one row.
  - THE 2027E STEP IN FOUNDRY SEGMENT REVENUE IS A RATIO ARTIFACT. The
    internal ratio goes from 38.2% (FY2026E, H2 at 38.0%) to the flat 36.0%
    forecast. So Foundry segment revenue grows only +6.8% in Base 2027E and
    falls 1.2% in Bear, even though Products grows 7.1% / 4.3%. Holding
    38.2% instead would add ~$1.4B of internal revenue in Base 2027E. Base
    2027E Foundry margin would then read -28.6% instead of -30.2%, with
    consolidated revenue and OI unchanged (Section A).
  - FOUNDRY MARGINS ARE NOW READABLE AGAINST HISTORY. Base runs -30.2%
    (2027E) to +4.4% (2036E), against -57.9% (FY2025) and -40.5% (H1'26)
    on the same gross-revenue definition. Bull reaches +11.4%; Bear
    improves only to -26.4%.
  - OPERATING-INCOME ELIMINATIONS STEP FROM +$975M (FY2026E) TO 0 (2027E).
    The actuals were (205) / (161) / +619 in FY2023-25 and +475 in H1'26.
    The zero is the main file's judgment and is kept here so consolidated
    OI ties. In all three scenarios consolidated OI is an INPUT (scenario
    margin x revenue). A non-zero elimination would therefore not change
    consolidated OI or value. It would shift the plug instead: Foundry OI
    in Bull and Bear, the margin round-down line in Base.
  - ALL OTHER MARGIN RUNS 8-13% IN THE FORECAST (flat $0.4B OI), against
    27.6% / -1.6% / 7.4% in FY2023-25 and 25.0% in H1'26.
