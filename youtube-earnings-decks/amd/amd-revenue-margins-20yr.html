FY2000 – FY2016  ·  GAAP, as reported

Seventeen fiscal years. Ten of them operating losses.

Fifty-one bars, every one an as-reported GAAP figure from AMD's own 10-K filings — no estimates, no interpolation. Revenue never once cleared $6.6B here, and FY2016's $4,272M finished below FY2000's $4,644M. Sum the seventeen reported operating-income lines and you get a cumulative $5,121M operating loss.

Structural breaks inside this window

  • FY2000 was a 53-week year; FY2001–FY2005 were 52.
  • Legerity sold Aug 2000. Spansion (Flash) consolidated Jun 2003, deconsolidated at its Dec 2005 IPO with no restatement — so FY2005 → FY2006 ($5,848M → $5,649M) is a mix change, not a decline.
  • ATI acquired Oct 2006 (~1 quarter of it in FY2006); GlobalFoundries spun off Mar 2009.
  • FY2012's $1,235M gross profit reflects large inventory / GlobalFoundries charges.

02 / The Zen Decade
FY2016 – FY2025  ·  GAAP, as reported
02  ·  Nasdaq: AMD  ·  FY2016 – FY2025

Same company, ten years later: revenue up 8×, gross profit up 17×.

FY2016 repeats from the previous slide as the pivot — same bars, same filings, a y-axis spanning 3.6× the dollar range. Revenue went $4,272M → $34,639M, gross profit $998M → $17,152M. FY2025's gross profit alone is 2.6× the highest revenue AMD reported in the seventeen years before it, and operating income has been positive every year since FY2017.

Why the amber bar sags in FY2022–FY2023Revenue and gross profit both hit records in FY2022, yet GAAP operating income fell to $1,264M and then $401M. That is not a one-time charge — it is recurring amortization of acquisition-related intangibles from Xilinx (closed 14 Feb 2022): $1,869M in operating expenses plus $942M in cost of sales in FY2023 alone. It is exactly the gap AMD's non-GAAP figures exclude. These bars are GAAP.

* Disclosed one-time items — bars stay as-reported, nothing netted out

  • FY24$186M restructuring charges, in operating expenses.
  • FY25~$440M of net inventory and related charges tied to the U.S. export control on Instinct MI308 — booked in cost of sales, so it hits the gross-profit bar too. FY2025's restructuring line is nil.
  • FY16–23Not catalogued — unstarred here means not checked, not clean. The one thing that is checked: FY2023's restructuring line is nil.

Structural notes & why FY2016 repeats

  • FY2016 sits on both slides so the cut is visible rather than hidden. Same filing, same figures — only the y-axis changes, and it spans 3.6× the dollar range.
  • Xilinx acquired Feb 2022 — it drives the revenue step-up and the amortization that suppresses FY2022–FY2023 GAAP operating income.
  • ZT Systems acquired 2025; its manufacturing business is discontinued operations in FY2025. Bars are continuing operations, and FY2024 / FY2023 were not recast by it.
  • FY2025 ended 27 Dec 2025; its 10-K, filed 4 Feb 2026, is the most recent annual filing. FY2026 is in progress and deliberately absent.
