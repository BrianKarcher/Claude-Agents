Nasdaq: AMD  ·  Twenty Fiscal Years

From a $2.9B operating loss to $3.7B in operating profit.

Sixty bars, twenty fiscal years, every one an as-reported GAAP figure from AMD's own 10-K filings — no estimates, no interpolation. Revenue barely moved from FY2006 to FY2019, then multiplied roughly five-fold in six years. Operating income sat below the zero line in seven of those twenty years.

  • FY06$416M in-process R&D charge from the ATI acquisition (FY2006 carries only ~one quarter of ATI).
  • FY07$1,608M goodwill / acquired-intangible impairment — the bulk of the −$2,865M operating loss.
  • FY08$1,089M impairment, $90M restructuring, $193M gain on sale of 200mm equipment.
  • FY09+$1,242M Intel legal-settlement credit (see note above).
  • FY24$186M restructuring charges.  FY25~$440M net inventory and related charges from the Instinct MI308 export control — booked in cost of sales, so it hits gross profit too.

Basis

  • GAAP, as originally reported in each fiscal year's own 10-K. AMD captions the line "Gross margin" through FY2018 and "Gross profit" from FY2019. Not AMD's non-GAAP figures, which run materially higher.
  • Later-recast alternates are not plotted: FY2006/FY2007 were recast for discontinued operations in the FY2008 10-K, and FY2016/FY2017 restated under ASC 606 in the FY2018 10-K. Both bases are as-filed; one consistent basis is used across all twenty years.
