## Reconciliation notes

- **All figures are GAAP as-reported.** AMD's investor materials headline non-GAAP gross margin and non-GAAP operating income, which run materially higher (they exclude amortization of acquisition-related intangibles and stock-based compensation). The Xilinx acquisition (closed 2022-02-14) makes this gap very large from FY2022 on — e.g. FY2022 GAAP operating income of 1,264 vs. a much larger non-GAAP figure. Never mix GAAP and non-GAAP on one chart without labeling.
- **Verification method.** FY2010–FY2025 figures come from SEC XBRL company-concept data, i.e. the tagged values in the filings themselves. Every one of those years is reported identically in **two or three independently filed 10-Ks** (each 10-K carries three comparative years), which is the cross-check. FY2006–FY2009 pre-date AMD's income-statement XBRL and were parsed from the 10-K HTML directly; each of those four years appears in two separate filings, and each was additionally footed against the filing's own quarterly table and expense lines.
- **Independent non-SEC cross-check.** stockanalysis.com confirms FY2021–FY2025 revenue / gross profit / operating income exactly. companiesmarketcap.com confirms revenue to 3 significant figures for FY2006 and FY2009–FY2016 and FY2018–FY2025. It **disagrees** on FY2007 ($5.93B), FY2008 ($5.87B) and FY2017 ($5.39B), none of which match either as-reported or restated SEC figures — treat that aggregator as unreliable for those three years and use the filings. See `extracts/AMD-AGGREGATOR-FY1996-FY2025.md`.
- **Structural breaks worth knowing when narrating this series:** ATI acquired 2006-10-25 (FY2006 has ~1 quarter of ATI); GlobalFoundries manufacturing spun off March 2009 (FY2009 operating income of 664 includes a **+1,242 legal-settlement credit** from Intel, without which FY2009 would have been an operating loss); FY2012's 1,235 gross profit reflects large inventory/GF-related charges; Xilinx acquired 2022-02-14 (drives the FY2022 revenue step-up and the amortization that suppresses FY2022–FY2023 GAAP operating income); ZT Systems acquired 2025, with the ZT manufacturing business classified as discontinued operations in FY2025 (revenue/gross profit/operating income above are continuing operations; FY2024 and FY2023 comparatives were **not** changed by it).
