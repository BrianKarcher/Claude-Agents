# AMD — Annual (Fiscal Year) Financials Cache (GAAP, $ millions)

Read this file first before fetching anything for AMD. Only fetch/verify a fiscal year that's missing or listed as a gap below.

**Basis convention:** each row is the figure **as originally reported in that fiscal year's own 10-K**. Where a later filing restated or recast a year (FY2006, FY2007 for discontinued operations; FY2016, FY2017 for ASC 606), the restated figure is recorded in the "Restated / recast alternates" section below — never blended into the row above. Both bases are as-filed; pick one and label it on any chart.

**Metric labels:** AMD's income statement calls gross profit **"Gross margin"** through FY2018 and **"Gross profit"** from FY2019 onward — same measure. AMD does not use the caption "Operating profit"; the line is **"Operating income (loss)"** (later just "Operating income"). That is the operating-profit metric in this table.

**FY2000–FY2005 have no gross-profit line at all.** In that era AMD's income statement runs Net sales → an "Expenses:" block (cost of sales, R&D, MG&A, restructuring) → total expenses → Operating income (loss). Gross profit for those six years is **derived as revenue − cost of sales**, an identity from two audited as-reported lines, and is cross-checked in every case against the gross margin **percentage** AMD discloses in its own MD&A. See the rounding note below for FY2002 and FY2004.

26 fiscal years, FY2000 through FY2025. **No gaps.** Every row is traceable to an SEC filing.

† **Derived gross profit (FY2000–FY2005 only).** These six values are revenue − cost of sales computed from the audited figures **in thousands**, then rounded to $ millions. AMD's own later Item 6 five-year tables print a dollar "Gross margin" line for FY2002–FY2005 that is computed by subtracting *already-rounded millions*, which differs by $1M in two years: AMD prints **592** for FY2002 (derived 591.368 → 591) and **1,968** for FY2004 (derived 1,968.850 → 1,969). FY2003 (1,192) and FY2005 (2,392) agree on both routes; FY2000 and FY2001 pre-date any AMD-printed gross-margin line. The table uses the derived-from-thousands value for all six years so the basis is consistent. Both numbers trace to filings — this is a rounding difference, not a discrepancy.

**Fiscal calendar changed inside this range.** Through FY2005 AMD used a 52-to-53-week fiscal year ending on the **last Sunday** in December; the later convention is the last Saturday. **FY2000 was a 53-week year** (ended 2000-12-31) — every other year FY1998–FY2005 was 52 weeks, so FY2000 carries one extra week of revenue and expense. FY2025 ended 2025-12-27; its 10-K (accession 0000002488-26-000018) was filed 2026-02-04 and is the most recent annual filing as of 2026-08-04. FY2026 is in progress — do not add a row until AMD files the FY2026 10-K.

- **As-originally-reported (the main table)** is the single consistent rule across all 26 years and is what AMD headlined at the time. Recommended default.
- If you instead want the ASC 606 basis for FY2016/FY2017 (comparable to FY2018+), use the alternates — but note FY2015 and earlier were never restated to ASC 606, so the series is still mixed-basis at the FY2015/FY2016 boundary. Say so in the caption either way.
- The FY2006/FY2007 discontinued-ops recast is the more comparable basis to FY2008+, because FY2008 onward is on a continuing-operations basis. If a slide leans on the FY2006→FY2008 collapse, prefer the recast alternates and label them.

## Reconciliation notes

- **All figures are GAAP as-reported.** AMD's investor materials headline non-GAAP gross margin and non-GAAP operating income, which run materially higher (they exclude amortization of acquisition-related intangibles and stock-based compensation). The Xilinx acquisition (closed 2022-02-14) makes this gap very large from FY2022 on — e.g. FY2022 GAAP operating income of 1,264 vs. a much larger non-GAAP figure. Never mix GAAP and non-GAAP on one chart without labeling.
- **Verification method.** FY2010–FY2025 figures come from SEC XBRL company-concept data, i.e. the tagged values in the filings themselves. Every one of those years is reported identically in **two or three independently filed 10-Ks** (each 10-K carries three comparative years), which is the cross-check. FY2006–FY2009 pre-date AMD's income-statement XBRL and were parsed from the 10-K HTML directly; each of those four years appears in two separate filings, and each was additionally footed against the filing's own quarterly table and expense lines. **FY2000–FY2005** were parsed from six 10-K HTML/text filings (FY2000 10-K405 through FY2005 10-K) and cross-checked the same way, with three independent checks per year: (a) each year appears identically in **three to five** separately filed annual reports, counting Item 6 five-year tables; (b) each year's unaudited quarterly columns sum to the annual figures; (c) each year's derived gross profit reconciles to the gross margin **percentage** AMD discloses in MD&A. The only footing exception is a **$1 thousand** rounding difference in the FY2005 unaudited quarterly MG&A/operating-income columns (sum 1,016,084 / 231,656 vs. audited 1,016,085 / 231,655) — noted in `extracts/AMD-10K-FY2005.md`.
- **Amendments checked.** AMD's FY2002 Form 10-K/A (accession 0001193125-03-056066, filed 2003-09-30) was opened: it amends **Item 14/15 exhibits only** and restates no financial statements. Nothing in FY2000–FY2005 is affected.
- **Independent non-SEC cross-check.** stockanalysis.com confirms FY2021–FY2025 revenue / gross profit / operating income exactly. companiesmarketcap.com confirms revenue to 3 significant figures for **FY2000–FY2005**, FY2006 and FY2009–FY2016 and FY2018–FY2025. It **disagrees** on FY2007 ($5.93B), FY2008 ($5.87B) and FY2017 ($5.39B), none of which match either as-reported or restated SEC figures — treat that aggregator as unreliable for those three years and use the filings. No aggregator supplies gross profit or operating income for FY2000–FY2005, so those rest entirely on the filings. See `extracts/AMD-AGGREGATOR-FY1996-FY2025.md`.
- **Structural breaks worth knowing when narrating this series:** Legerity (voice comms) sold 2000-08-04, so FY2000 revenue includes ~7 months of it and FY2001 none; **FY2000 was a 53-week year**, every other year FY1998–FY2005 was 52 weeks; **Spansion (Flash memory) consolidated from 2003-06-30** — FY2003 has ~6 months of the combined AMD/Fujitsu memory business, FY2004 and FY2005 have full years, and AMD's own Item 6 footnote warns FY2004 "is not comparable to 2003, nor is 2003 data completely comparable to prior years' data"; **Spansion deconsolidated 2005-12-21** at its IPO (60% → 37.9%), so FY2005 is the **last year containing Flash memory revenue and FY2006 contains none** — the FY2005 → FY2006 move (5,848 → 5,649) is a change of business mix, not a decline, and prior years were *not* restated; ATI acquired 2006-10-25 (FY2006 has ~1 quarter of ATI); GlobalFoundries manufacturing spun off March 2009 (FY2009 operating income of 664 includes a **+1,242 legal-settlement credit** from Intel, without which FY2009 would have been an operating loss); FY2012's 1,235 gross profit reflects large inventory/GF-related charges; Xilinx acquired 2022-02-14 (drives the FY2022 revenue step-up and the amortization that suppresses FY2022–FY2023 GAAP operating income); ZT Systems acquired 2025, with the ZT manufacturing business classified as discontinued operations in FY2025 (revenue/gross profit/operating income above are continuing operations; FY2024 and FY2023 comparatives were **not** changed by it).
