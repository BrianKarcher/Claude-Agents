## Notes

- **"Operating profit" is not a caption AMD uses.** The income-statement line is literally **"Operating income (loss)"**; that is the metric captured here and in the cache table.
- **Gross profit is captioned "Gross margin"** on the face of the statement in this era. AMD renamed the line to "Gross profit" beginning with the FY2019 10-K. Same measure, different label.
- **FY2005 revenue presentation:** the statement splits FY2005 into "Net revenue $4,972" plus "Net revenue from related party $876" = "Total net revenue $5,848". The 5,848 total is the comparable figure. FY2006/FY2007 have no related-party revenue line.
- **FY2006 includes only a partial year of ATI.** AMD closed the ATI Technologies acquisition on 2006-10-25, so FY2006 contains roughly one quarter of ATI and FY2007 is the first full year. The $416M in-process R&D charge and the FY2007 $1,608M goodwill/intangible impairment both relate to that acquisition. This is why FY2006 gross margin (2,793) exceeds FY2007 (2,262) on higher FY2007 revenue.
- **These figures were later recast.** In the FY2008 10-K, AMD reclassified the Handheld and Digital TV businesses into discontinued operations and restated 2006 and 2007. The recast figures (FY2007: 5,858 / 2,189 / (2,310); FY2006: 5,627 / 2,794 / (17)) are in `AMD-10K-FY2008.md`. Do not mix the two bases on one chart without labeling.
