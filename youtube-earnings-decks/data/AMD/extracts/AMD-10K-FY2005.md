## Notes

- **The single most important structural fact about FY2005: it is the last year that includes the Flash memory business.** AMD consolidated Spansion's results through **2005-12-20**; Spansion's IPO on 2005-12-21 diluted AMD from 60% to 37.9%, after which AMD used the equity method. FY2005 therefore contains ~360 of 364 days of Memory Products revenue, while **FY2006 contains none**. Combined with the ATI acquisition closing 2006-10-25, the FY2005 → FY2006 comparison (5,848 → 5,649) is a change of business mix, not a revenue decline in any comparable sense. AMD did **not** restate FY2005 or earlier to remove Spansion — the FY2006, FY2007 and FY2008 10-K Item 6 tables all still show FY2005 revenue of 5,848. **Any chart crossing the FY2005/FY2006 boundary must annotate this break.**
- **FY2005 operating income of 231,655 includes the $16M Spansion goodwill write-off buried in MG&A.** It is not broken out on the face of the statement. Do not present an ex-write-off FY2005 figure as GAAP; if the slide compares FY2004 → FY2005 operating income (221.8 → 231.7, a thin +4.4%), note that the $16M charge is inside the FY2005 number.
- **FY2005 net income of 165,483 is not a clean read either** — it is struck after the $109.7M Spansion dilution loss and after +$125.2M of minority interest in Spansion's losses. Revenue / gross profit / operating income are the comparable metrics for this era.
- **$1M rounding artifacts on gross profit in two years.** Derived from the audited thousands: FY2004 = 1,968,850 → **1,969** ($M) and FY2002 = 591,368 → **591** ($M). AMD's own Item 6 tables print **1,968** and **592**, because they subtract already-rounded millions (5,001 − 3,033; 2,697 − 2,105). The cache uses the values derived from the audited thousands and flags the difference. FY2003 (1,192) and FY2005 (2,392) agree on both routes.
- **Fiscal calendar.** 52-to-53-week year ending the last Sunday in December. Fiscal 2005 (December 25), 2004 (December 26) and 2003 (December 28) were all 52-week years.
- Metric-label note: caption is "Operating income (loss)"; there is no gross-margin caption on the face of the statement in this era. AMD's first printed dollar gross-margin line covering these years is the FY2006 10-K's Item 6 table.
