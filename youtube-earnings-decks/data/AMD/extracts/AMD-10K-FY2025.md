## Notes

- All figures **GAAP**. AMD's own earnings materials headline non-GAAP gross margin and non-GAAP operating income, which exclude the acquisition-related intangible amortization shown above (a combined $2,254M in FY2025 across COGS and opex) plus stock-based compensation. The GAAP/non-GAAP gap is large post-Xilinx; never present one as the other.
- **FY2025 discontinued operations = the ZT Manufacturing Business.** Net income from discontinued operations of $66M (net of $54M tax) includes that business's results plus a $121M change in fair value of a contingent-consideration liability. Revenue, gross profit and operating income above are continuing operations. Importantly, the FY2024 and FY2023 comparatives here are **identical** to what those years' own 10-Ks reported — this reclassification did not recast prior-year revenue / gross profit / operating income.
- MD&A attributes FY2025 gross margin expansion to product mix, partially offset by approximately **$440M of net inventory and related charges tied to the U.S. government export control on AMD Instinct MI308** products.
- Income-statement captions in this era are "Gross profit" and "Operating income" (AMD dropped the "(loss)" suffix and renamed "Gross margin" to "Gross profit" starting with the FY2019 10-K).
