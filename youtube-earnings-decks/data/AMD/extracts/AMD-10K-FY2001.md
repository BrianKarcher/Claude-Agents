## Notes

- **FY2001's operating loss of (58,258) is entirely a restructuring artifact.** Excluding the $89.3M 2001 Restructuring Plan charge, FY2001 operations were around break-even to modestly positive. The as-reported GAAP loss is the figure in the cache; if a slide narrates FY2001 as "AMD's first operating loss of the downturn," say that the charge is what tipped it negative. Do **not** publish an ex-restructuring FY2001 figure as if it were GAAP.
- The whole charge landed in **Q3 2001** — the quarterly operating loss of (229,594) that quarter is not an organic run-rate.
- **Fiscal calendar.** 52-to-53-week year ending the last Sunday in December. Fiscal 2001 (ended December 30) and 1999 were 52-week years; **fiscal 2000 was a 53-week year** ended December 31, 2000. The FY2000 → FY2001 revenue decline of 16% is therefore flattered slightly by the extra week in the base year.
- Metric-label note: caption is "Operating income (loss)"; there is no gross-margin caption on the face of the statement in this era.
