## Notes

- **FY2003's restructuring line is a net RECOVERY of $13.9M, not a charge** — it makes the operating loss $13.9M *smaller* than operations alone. This is a sign-flip trap: a chart that shades "restructuring charge" bars must not treat FY2003 as a charge year. Excluding the recovery, the FY2003 operating loss would have been about (247,277).
- **Major structural break: Spansion is consolidated from 2003-06-30.** AMD and Fujitsu combined their Flash memory businesses into FASL LLC (later Spansion) on that date; AMD held 60% and **consolidated** it, where the predecessor joint venture had previously been accounted for under the equity method. So FY2003 contains roughly **six months** of the combined memory business and FY2004 contains twelve. AMD's own Item 6 footnote in the FY2005 10-K says FY2004 "is not comparable to 2003, nor is 2003 data completely comparable to prior years' data." Any chart spanning FY2002 → FY2004 must annotate this — the revenue growth is substantially inorganic.
- The new **"Net sales to related party"** line (448,940 in FY2003) is Spansion's sales to Fujitsu. It is part of total net sales; the 3,519,168 total is the comparable revenue figure.
- **Fiscal calendar.** 52-week year ended December 28, 2003.
- Metric-label note: caption is "Operating income (loss)"; there is no gross-margin caption on the face of the statement in this era.
