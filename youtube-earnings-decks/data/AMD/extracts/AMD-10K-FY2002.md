## Notes

- **FY2002 is the trough of the dot-com bust for AMD and the largest operating loss in this era: (1,225,386).** Of that, $330.6M is the 2002 Restructuring Plan charge and a further $42M sits inside R&D as a one-off development-services payment — together ~30% of the loss. The as-reported GAAP figure is what belongs on a chart; if the slide narrates the loss, annotate the restructuring charge rather than netting it out.
- Q4 2002 alone carries the entire restructuring charge, so the (589,995) fourth-quarter operating loss is not a run-rate.
- **Revenue fell 30.7% in FY2002**, the steepest decline in the FY1998–FY2005 range, driven by lower unit demand and ASPs for both PC processors and Flash memory.
- **Fiscal calendar.** 52-to-53-week year ending the last Sunday in December. Fiscal 2002 (ended December 29) and 2001 were 52-week years; **fiscal 2000 was a 53-week year** ended December 31, 2000.
- **Segment/structure note:** FY2002 pre-dates the Spansion consolidation. AMD's Flash memory business at this point is AMD's own, with FASL (the Fujitsu joint venture) accounted for under the **equity method** and reported below the operating line as "equity in net income of joint venture." From 2003-06-30 that changes — see `AMD-10K-FY2003.md`.
- Metric-label note: caption is "Operating income (loss)"; there is no gross-margin caption on the face of the statement in this era.
