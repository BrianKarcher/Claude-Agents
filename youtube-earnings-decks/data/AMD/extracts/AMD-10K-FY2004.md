## Notes

- FY2004 is AMD's **return to operating profit** after two loss years, at 221,809. The $5.5M restructuring charge is immaterial to that; FY2004's operating income is essentially clean.
- **FY2004 is the first full year of consolidated Spansion.** AMD consolidated the combined AMD/Fujitsu Flash memory business from 2003-06-30, so FY2004 has twelve months against FY2003's six. AMD's own Item 6 footnote states FY2004 "is not comparable to 2003." The 42% FY2003 → FY2004 revenue growth is substantially inorganic — annotate it on any chart.
- **Later filings reclassify FY2003/FY2004 operating expenses.** The FY2007 10-K's Item 6 table folds the restructuring line into marketing, general and administrative: it shows FY2004 MG&A of 812 ($M) = 807.0 + 5.5, and FY2003 MG&A of 573 = 587.3 − 13.9. **Revenue, cost of sales, gross margin and operating income are unchanged** by that reclassification; only the expense split moves. The as-originally-reported split above is what belongs in the cache.
- **A $1M rounding artifact to be aware of on FY2004 gross profit.** Derived from the audited thousands, FY2004 gross profit is 1,968,850 → **1,969** ($M). AMD's own Item 6 tables in the FY2006 and FY2007 10-Ks print **1,968**, because they subtract already-rounded millions (5,001 − 3,033). The cache uses 1,969 (the correct rounding of the audited figures) and flags the difference. Both trace to filings; neither is an error worth restating.
- **Fiscal calendar.** 52-week year ended December 26, 2004.
- Metric-label note: caption is "Operating income (loss)"; there is no gross-margin caption on the face of the statement in this era.
