## Notes

- **Which gross margin to use in a peer comp:** Snowflake reports two GAAP gross margins. **GAAP total gross margin is 66.6%**; GAAP *product* gross margin is 71.0%. The higher 71% figure excludes the loss-making professional-services line. For an apples-to-apples comparison against NVDA's and PLTR's total-company GAAP gross margin, use **66.6%** and label it "GAAP, total revenue." If the 71.0% product figure is used instead, it must be labeled "GAAP product gross margin" on the slide — the two are not interchangeable.
- **Non-GAAP figures in the same release that must NOT be substituted:** non-GAAP gross margin 72%, non-GAAP product gross margin 75%, non-GAAP product gross profit $1,002,695K, non-GAAP operating income $165,798K (11.9% margin). Snowflake is **GAAP operating-loss-making** this quarter (−$326.2M) while non-GAAP operating income is positive $165.8M; presenting the non-GAAP number as if it were GAAP would materially misstate profitability.
- Non-GAAP exclusions per the release: stock-based-compensation-related charges (incl. employer payroll tax on employee stock transactions), amortization of acquired intangibles, acquisition/strategic-investment expenses, amortization of debt issuance costs, restructuring, office-facility-exit impairment, noncontrolling-interest adjustments, and related tax effects.
- Non-financial key metrics reported (usable, from this primary source): net revenue retention rate **126%**; **779** customers with TTM product revenue > $1M (up 29% YoY); **813** Forbes Global 2000 customers; remaining performance obligations **$9.21B**, up 38% YoY; 616 net new customers added in the quarter.
- **Forward-looking (do NOT put in the cache table; label clearly if used in a deck):** Q2 FY2027 guidance of product revenue $1,415–1,420M (+30% YoY) and non-GAAP operating margin 12.5%; FY2027 guidance of product revenue $5,840M (+31% YoY, raised from prior $5,660M / 27%), non-GAAP product gross margin 75.0%, non-GAAP operating margin 13.5%, non-GAAP adjusted FCF margin 23.0%. All guidance is **non-GAAP** and is product revenue, not total revenue — do not compare it directly against total-revenue actuals.
- Announced in the quarter (context, not financials): $6B multi-year AWS agreement, deepened OpenAI partnership, definitive agreement (May 2026) to acquire Natoma. The Natoma acquisition may affect future-period comparability.
- Fiscal calendar: Snowflake's FY2027 Q1 ended **April 30, 2026** (FY ends Jan 31). Different from both PLTR (calendar quarters) and NVDA (FY ends late Jan, quarter ended April 26, 2026). Label periods per company.
