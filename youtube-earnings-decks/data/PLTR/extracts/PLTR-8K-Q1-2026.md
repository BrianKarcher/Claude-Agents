## Notes
- **GAAP vs. adjusted:** "Adjusted income from operations," "adjusted operating margin," "adjusted free cash flow," "adjusted EBITDA," and "adjusted EPS" are Palantir-defined non-GAAP measures excluding stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes (adjusted EPS also excludes income tax effects/adjustments at an assumed 23.0% long-term effective rate). Never present them as GAAP. Note the unusual inversion this quarter: **GAAP diluted EPS ($0.34) is HIGHER than adjusted diluted EPS ($0.33)**, because the non-GAAP tax adjustment (−$243,624K) exceeds the SBC add-backs. A deck must not describe adjusted EPS as the "beat" figure without saying this.
- **Adjusted gross margin is NOT in this press release.** It is only in the Q1 2026 Business Update investor presentation (88%) — see `PLTR-INVESTORDECK-Q1-2026.md`.
- **Plain (unadjusted) free cash flow** is not a metric Palantir publishes. CFO − capex = 899,165 − 7,401 = **891,764** for Q1 2026 and 310,263 − 6,184 = **304,079** for Q1 2025. These are arithmetic on two reported line items; if used, label as "operating cash flow less capex," not as the company's "adjusted free cash flow" ($924,630 / $370,377), which is a different, larger number.
- The US commercial / US government split is disclosed only to the nearest $1 million in the press release and investor deck; the exact-thousands split is not broken out in the 10-Q (the 10-Q's segment note splits worldwide Commercial $774,173 / Government $858,410, a different cut). Do not present $595M / $687M with implied precision beyond $1M.
- Revenue/gross profit/operating income here confirm the existing `../quarterly-financials.md` Q1 2026 row (1,633.00 / 1,417.00 / 754.00) — cache row validated against the primary source, no correction needed.
