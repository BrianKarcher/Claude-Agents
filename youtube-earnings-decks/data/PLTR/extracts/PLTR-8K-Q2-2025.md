## Notes
- **This confirms the $0.16 adjusted diluted EPS figure** that a deck slide previously cited without a source. It is a **non-GAAP** figure; GAAP diluted EPS for the same quarter was $0.13. If a slide compares it to Q1 2026's $0.33 adjusted EPS, both are adjusted and the comparison is apples-to-apples — but note the two quarters are not adjacent and are not the same fiscal quarter.
- Note the customer-count growth stated here (+43% Y/Y) is **total** customer count growth, and it is stated as a percentage only — no absolute count is given in the press release. The absolute TTM counts are in the investor presentation (849 total / 692 commercial / 485 US commercial as of TTM ended Jun 30, 2025) — see `PLTR-INVESTORDECK-Q1-2026.md`.
- Plain CFO − capex for Q2 2025 = 539,251 − 7,634 = **531,617**; the company's adjusted FCF of 568,769 is a different, larger figure.
