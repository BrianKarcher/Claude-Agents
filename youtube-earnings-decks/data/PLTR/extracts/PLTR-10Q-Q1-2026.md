## Notes
- The **1,007 customer count is TOTAL customers (commercial + government), not commercial customers.** An AI search summary encountered while researching this asserted "1,007 commercial customers" — that is a misattribution. The 10-Q's own definition explicitly includes government agencies (each separately-contracting sub-agency counted separately). Commercial-only TTM customer count for the same period is 832 (investor deck).
- The 10-Q's segment split (Commercial $774,173 / Government $858,410) is **worldwide** and is a different cut from the press release's **US-only** commercial ($595M) / government ($687M) split. Do not mix the two on one slide.
- 87%/88% gross margins here are the company's own rounded MD&A figures; the exact GAAP computation is 1,416,785 / 1,632,583 = 86.78%.
- Shares outstanding are as of April 27, 2026 — do **not** use that count to derive a market cap for a later date without saying so; see `PLTR-MARKETDATA-2026-07-31.md`.
