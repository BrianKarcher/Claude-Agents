## Notes

- All figures are **GAAP** (as-reported), not Palantir's "Adjusted" (non-GAAP) metrics. Palantir's own investor materials typically headline Adjusted gross margin / Adjusted operating margin, which run higher than these GAAP figures (Adjusted excludes stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes). Do not mix GAAP and Adjusted figures on the same chart without labeling which is which.
- Q4 2024's operating income ($11.04M) looks anomalous next to Q3 2024 ($113.14M) despite higher revenue — this was verified via the FY2024 total reconciliation above, not just taken on faith from the aggregator; it is a real, large quarter-over-quarter margin compression, not a scraping error.
- Q1 2021 (quarter ended March 31, 2021) is **not included** in this aggregator's table — it starts at Q2 2021. See `PLTR-8K-Q1-2021-revenue.md` for what was independently verified for that quarter, and the gap note in `../quarterly-financials.md`.
