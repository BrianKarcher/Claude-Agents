## Notes

- **Recast basis.** This filing moves the Handheld and Digital TV businesses into discontinued operations "for all periods presented," so the 2006 and 2007 columns here differ from what those years' own 10-Ks reported. Revenue: 5,627 vs. 5,649 (FY2006) and 5,858 vs. 6,013 (FY2007). Operating loss: (17) vs. (47) for FY2006 and (2,310) vs. (2,865) for FY2007 — the FY2007 gap is large because much of the ATI-related goodwill/intangible impairment was reassigned to discontinued operations. Both bases are as-filed; neither is a correction of the other.
- FY2008 itself is **not** affected by a later recast: the FY2009 and FY2010 10-Ks both carry FY2008 at 5,808 / 2,320 / (1,955).
- FY2008 is pre-GlobalFoundries. AMD spun its manufacturing operations into GlobalFoundries in March 2009, so FY2008 and earlier are on a fully-integrated-manufacturer basis and FY2009 onward progressively are not.
- Caption note: "Gross margin" is AMD's label for gross profit in this era; "Operating income (loss)" is the operating-profit line.
