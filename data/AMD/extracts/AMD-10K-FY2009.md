## Notes

- **FY2009's positive operating income of 664 is driven by a one-time +$1,242M legal-settlement credit**, the Intel antitrust settlement recorded as a credit within operating expenses. Backing it out, FY2009 operations were a loss of roughly (578). Any slide highlighting FY2009's "return to operating profit" must say this — it is not an operating turnaround.
- FY2009 is the GlobalFoundries transition year: AMD spun off manufacturing into GlobalFoundries in March 2009, so FY2009 is a partial year on the new fab-lite basis.
- Interest expense and other income lines for FY2008/FY2007 differ from the FY2008 10-K presentation (this filing shows FY2008 interest expense (391) vs. (366) previously), a below-the-operating-line reclassification. Revenue, gross margin and operating income are unaffected.
