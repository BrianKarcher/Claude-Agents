# Intel — Q1 2026 Financial Results (extract)

Source: Intel Q1 2026 earnings press release, https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1767/intel-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial-results (pulled live via web, Sept 27, 2026). Quarter ended March 28, 2026.

## Income statement (Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025)

**GAAP**
- Revenue: $13.6B (+7% YoY); actual $13.58B vs consensus $12.42B (beat)
- Gross margin: 39.4% (+2.5 ppts YoY)
- Operating loss: $(3.1)B
- Net loss attributable to Intel: $(3.7)B
- EPS (diluted): $(0.73)

**Non-GAAP**
- Gross margin: 41.0% (+1.8 ppts YoY)
- Operating income: $1.7B
- Net income attributable to Intel: $1.5B (+156% YoY)
- EPS (diluted): $0.29 (+123% YoY) vs consensus $0.01 (large beat)
