# Intel — Q2 2026 Financial Results (extract)

Source: Intel Q2 2026 earnings press release, https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1776/intel-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results (pulled live via web, Sept 27, 2026). Quarter ended June 27, 2026.

## Income statement (Q2 2026 vs Q2 2025)

**GAAP**
- Revenue: $16,128M (+25% YoY, vs $12,859M) — strongest YoY growth in 15+ years
- Gross profit: $6,509M vs $3,542M
- Gross margin: 40.4% vs 27.5%
- Operating income (loss): $1,796M vs $(3,176)M
- Net income (loss) attributable to Intel: $(11,033)M vs $(2,918)M — large GAAP net loss despite positive operating income (tax valuation-allowance / one-time items; non-GAAP net income was positive, see below)
- EPS (diluted): $(2.16) vs $(0.67)

**Non-GAAP**
- Gross margin: 41.8% vs 29.7%
- Operating income: $2,770M vs $(503)M
- Net income attributable to Intel: $2,197M vs $(441)M
- EPS (diluted): $0.42 vs $(0.10)

## Segment revenue & operating income (Q2 2026 vs Q2 2025)

| Segment | Q2'26 Rev | Q2'25 Rev | Q2'26 OpInc/Loss | Q2'25 OpInc/Loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCG (Client Computing) | $8,877M (+13%) | $7,871M | $2,343M | $2,053M |
| DCAI (Data Center & AI) | $6,262M (+59%) | $3,939M | $2,474M | $633M |
| Intel Foundry | $5,765M | $4,417M | $(2,089)M | $(3,168)M |
| All Other (incl. Mobileye) | $701M | $1,053M | $230M | $69M |

Note: Intel Foundry segment revenue is dominated by INTERNAL (intersegment) sales — Intel Products buys wafers from Intel Foundry at transfer prices. This is eliminated in consolidation. True external (third-party) Foundry revenue is a small fraction of the segment total — see INTC-STRATEGIC-CONTEXT-2026.md.

Altera was deconsolidated Sept 12, 2025 (51% stock sale); no longer a reportable segment.

- Total current assets: $57,213M vs $63,688M
- PP&E, net: $105,741M vs $105,414M
- Total assets: $202,439M vs $211,429M

- Adjusted free cash flow: $(8,419)M vs $(1,050)M

## Q3 2026 guidance

| Metric | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $15.8B–$16.8B |
| Gross margin (GAAP) | ~41.0% |
| Gross margin (Non-GAAP) | ~42.0% |

CEO Lip-Bu Tan: "our strongest revenue growth in more than fifteen years," driven by AI infrastructure demand (DCAI +59% YoY) and higher 18A factory yields / faster production cycles.

Also: Intel raised its full-year 2026 gross capex guidance to **>$20B** (from an earlier $18B) during this release, citing stronger Foundry customer demand signals, and flagged 2027 capex will be "significantly above" 2026 (see INTC-STRATEGIC-CONTEXT-2026.md).
