## Q4 2025 income statement

**GAAP**: Revenue $13.7B (-4% YoY vs $14.3B), gross margin 36.1% (vs 39.2%), operating income $580M (vs $412M, +41%), net loss $(591)M (vs $(126)M), EPS $(0.12) (vs $(0.03)).
**Non-GAAP**: Gross margin 37.9% (vs 42.1%), operating income $1.2B (vs $1.4B, -12%), net income $767M (vs $568M, +35%), EPS $0.15 (vs $0.13, +15%).

## Full-year 2025 segment revenue & operating income

| Segment | Rev FY25 | Rev FY24 | Chg | OpInc/Loss FY25 | OpInc/Loss FY24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCG | $32.2B | $33.3B | -3% | $9.3B | $11.6B |
| DCAI | $16.9B | $16.1B | +5% | $3.4B | $1.4B |
| **Total Intel Products** | $49.1B | $49.5B | -1% | $12.7B | $13.0B |
| Intel Foundry | $17.8B | $17.3B | +3% | $(10.3)B | $(13.3)B |
| All Other | $3.6B | $3.6B | -1% | $264M | $(57)M |

Note: Total Intel Products ($49.1B) + Intel Foundry ($17.8B) + All Other ($3.6B) = $70.5B, vs consolidated revenue of $52.9B — the ~$17.6B gap is the intersegment elimination (Intel Foundry's "revenue" is overwhelmingly internal wafer sales to Intel Products, which nets out in consolidation). See INTC-STRATEGIC-CONTEXT-2026.md for the external-vs-internal Foundry revenue split.

Altera included in "All Other" only through its Sept 12, 2025 deconsolidation (51% stock sale). Network and Edge Group (NEX) was folded into CCG and DCAI in Q1 2025 (no longer separate).
