# Tesla Robotaxi — Cities Currently Operating

_As of 2026-09-06. Scope: Tesla's own Robotaxi service (not Waymo/Zoox/others)._

Tesla Robotaxi is carrying public riders in **7 US metros**: Austin, Dallas, Houston,
Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and the San Francisco Bay Area. Six of the seven run with no
in-car person; the Bay Area still uses a safety driver because California regulates the
service as a chartered limousine, not a robotaxi.

Every "city" is really a set of mapped geofence zones, not the whole metro — a city on
the list does not mean full airport, downtown, or suburb coverage.

**Fleet context:** Texas vehicle registrations total ~420 (≈375 Model Y + 45 Cybercab) as
of early September 2026. Florida and Bay Area fleet sizes are not disclosed. The genuinely
unsupervised, no-monitor fleet across all cities is still only in the low tens.

## Quick read for city-by-city modeling

- **Unsupervised, permissive-state metros** (TX: Austin/Dallas/Houston; FL: Miami/Orlando/Tampa)
  are the near-term growth engine. All six went driverless with little or no supervised phase,
  which is the pattern to extrapolate for AZ and NV next.
- **California** is a capacity sink, not a growth driver — safety driver required, limo
  classification, slow path to true driverless.
- **Service areas are tiny** (4–20 sq mi in Florida). Fleet-size growth will track
  geofence expansion and new-metro adds, not within-metro saturation, for the next 1–2 years.
- **Cybercab** is only in one small Austin zone so far; treat mid-2026 as the start of the
  purpose-built-vehicle ramp, not volume.
