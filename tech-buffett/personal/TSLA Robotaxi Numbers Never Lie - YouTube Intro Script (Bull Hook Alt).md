## Production notes

- **Why this hook works:** it leads with the biggest number in the model, then turns it around in the
  same breath. The viewer stays to learn whether $4.6T or $171B is right. The "$43" version leads with
  the skeptic's number; this one leads with the bull's number and earns the skepticism later. Same
  facts, opposite emotional entry point.
- **Must-keep honesty beats (don't cut these for time):** (1) the caption "Bull case, 2036" on the
  opening number, and (2) "discount it back" at 0:45. Without them, $4.6T reads as a present-day price
  target, which the model does not say. Comments will catch it.
- **Thumbnail pairing:** "$4.6 TRILLION?" with a Cybercab, or "$4.6T vs $171B". Avoid putting
  "$43" in the thumbnail with this script, since it gives away the turn.
- **Number fact-check (all from the Oct 14 DCF, Bull case):**
  - **$4.6T** = Bull 2036 terminal value by exit multiple: 2036 EBITDA $285.17B × 16x = **$4,562.7B**,
    in 2036 dollars before discounting. "Four and a half trillion" in the setup is the same figure
    rounded down.
  - Cross-check: Sept multiples file, Bull 2036 $344.33B × 13x = $4,476.3B. Both are ~$4.5T.
  - The perpetuity-growth method gives a lower 2036 Bull value of **$2,534B**. If a commenter asks,
    the honest range for "Bull, valued in 2036" is **$2.5–4.6T**, and the script uses the exit-
    multiple end. Consider "two and a half to four and a half trillion" if you want to be bulletproof.
  - "Three times Tesla today": $4.56T / $1.48T = **3.1x**.
  - 4M Cybercabs/yr production in 2036; avg fleet **10.5M**, end-of-year fleet **11.85M** ("more than
    ten million").
  - Bull 2036 revenue **$659.9B** vs Uber Mobility ~$116B annualized (Q2 2026 $29.0B) = **5.7x**
    ("about five and a half times" is the conservative wording).
  - Present value: Bull EV **$1,414.7B** vs TSLA market cap **$1,482B** (95.5%).
  - Base EV **$171.3B** = 11.6% of market cap ("under twelve percent").
  - Bull probability weight: **20%**.
- **No $16T figure exists in any of the robotaxi files** (checked 2026-10-15: this DCF, the Sept
  segment DCF, and the Sept multiples file). Don't use $16T on screen unless it comes from a new,
  documented model.
