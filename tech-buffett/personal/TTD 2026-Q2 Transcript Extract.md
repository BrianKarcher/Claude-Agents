# The Trade Desk (TTD) — Q2 FY2026 Earnings Call Extract

- **Source:** `TTD 2026-Q2 Transcript.txt`
- **Call participants:** Jeff Green (CEO & Co-Founder), Nate Olmstead (CFO, joined ~1 month prior), Chris Toth (VP Investor Relations)

## Headline

Revenue growth decelerated sharply and management explicitly called results "below our expectations and below the standard we hold ourselves to." Two causes cited: (1) macro pressure concentrated in CPG and Auto (~25% of the business combined), driven by tariffs, oil prices, and a bifurcated consumer (affluent spending fine, lower-income squeezed); (2) self-acknowledged execution shortfalls, being addressed via a wave of new senior leadership hires and product upgrades.

## Platform Operations Expense Driver

Management attributed the rise in platform operations expense (26% of revenue in Q2'26 vs 22% in Q2'25) to an ongoing multi-year transition of workloads from third-party cloud to owned data centers, plus more AI-powered tooling — framed as near-term margin drag in exchange for longer-term efficiency/leverage.

## Growth / Retention Signals Cited by Management

- **Joint Business Plans (JBPs):** 217 clients as of Q2, +38% YoY. Revenue under JBPs growing **6x faster** than overall company revenue — called out repeatedly as the most bullish internal metric.
- Majority of **top-100 accounts** growing double digits YoY.
- Advertisers outside the top-500 growing **>50% YoY YTD** ("green shoots" from mid-market/challenger brands).
- A dedicated win-back/growth team grew its book of business **+250% YoY**, the fastest-growing team in business development.
- Retail media: partner retailers now represent **>80% of US retail sales**, including a renewed Walmart partnership.
- Case study: General Mills/Nature Valley UK campaign (Tesco, Sainsbury's, Ocado data + Koa AI) drove **5x sales uplift, 92% lower CPM, 2x ROAS** vs. baseline over four months.
- Audience Unlimited (in open beta): a global advertiser cut cost-per-unique-household and data CPM by **>25% each** versus a prior campaign.

## Product Roadmap

- **Measurement framework** (in alpha): aims to fix broken last-click/last-view attribution industry-wide; being built in partnership with major media, measurement, and data companies.
- **Audience Unlimited**: subscription-style, AI-driven third-party data access ("what Spotify did to music") — moving from limited early access to open beta.
- **"Zuma"** platform usability upgrade launching later in the month covered by the call — improved navigation, workflows, AI integration, UX.
- **Enterprise Kokai**: larger clients pre-negotiating features and using the full product suite, often via JBPs.
- Agentic AI positioned as complementary to, not a replacement for, DSP decisioning — management drew a sharp distinction between "decision buying" (TTD's model) and "Programmatic Guaranteed / fixed price" approaches it associates with commoditized, lower-value competition.

## Leadership Changes Discussed

New hires: **Nate Olmstead** (CFO, ex-Penguin Solutions/Logitech/HPE), **Vivek Kundra** (COO), **Kristi Argyilan** (Chief Commercial Officer, ex-Uber advertising head — focused on data partnerships, Audience Unlimited, measurement), **Ron Lamprecht** (Chief Business Development Officer, ex-Amazon), **Sarah Gavin** (CMO), **Vinny Rinaldi** (VP Client Strategy & Growth, ex-Hershey's). Several new board members referenced but not individually named. Management framed this as a deliberate infusion of large-company operational discipline, and asked investors for "runway" for these leaders to ramp.

## Analyst Q&A — Key Exchanges

- **Priorities to stabilize the business (Shyam Patil, Susquehanna):** Green named three: (1) ship the Zuma usability upgrade and continue Kokai/AI improvements, (2) ramp measurement and Audience Unlimited, (3) get the large wave of new leadership up to speed. Framed all three as controllable regardless of macro.
- **DSP relevance in an AI world (Matt Swanson, RBC):** Green argued AI does not disrupt the DSP model — "it is AI." Objectivity/data-trust is positioned as TTD's moat versus owned-and-operated platforms (Google, Amazon) that reuse client data across their own inventory and competitors. Called agentic-wrapped ad-network approaches from competitors a repeat of "ad networks of 2006."
- **Long-term profitability framework given weaker outlook (Vasily Karasyov, Cannonball Research):** Olmstead said the company will keep investing with conviction where it sees attractive returns while being equally disciplined elsewhere; no new profitability framework given yet ("stay tuned").
- **Pricing / take rate (Justin Patterson, KeyBanc):** Take rate has stayed in a narrow band for 10 years (up 5 years, down 5). Company philosophy: "not the cheapest, the best" — price changes only considered if they clearly drive more value/growth. Biggest opportunity seen is simplifying pricing structure, not cutting the net rate.
- **Guide implies ~12% Q3 revenue decline — controllable vs. uncontrollable factors, and Publicis relationship (Youssef Squali, Truist):** Green split the answer into product/team execution (controllable, already invested in) vs. macro (not controllable but broad-based tailwinds exist elsewhere). On Publicis: prior public dispute called "behind us," relationship framed as strong, with JBPs increasingly co-developed with agencies rather than in tension with them; cited a joint white-label Audience Unlimited / Agentic AI roadmap with agency partners.
- **New leadership hires — specifics (Tim Nollen, SSR):** Green highlighted Kristi Argyilan (retail media pioneer, ex-Uber ads), Ron Lamprecht (ex-Amazon, senior enterprise deal structuring), and Vinny Rinaldi (ex-Hershey's, advocate against "cheap reach" media buying) as examples of the caliber of hire being brought in.
- **Walled gardens / Amazon DSP / Google buyer-direct competition (Dan Salmon, New Street Research):** Green argued no single platform will dominate ("an auction with only one bidder isn't an auction"), that TTD already leads the open-internet programmatic space, and reframed the platform-fee comparison: a competitor's lower headline take rate doesn't mean cheaper media once bundled costs are considered (his example: TTD at 8% netting $1.08 per $1 of media vs. a competitor at 4% netting $1.04, with the real question being which impression was better for the brand). Walled gardens are structurally incentivized to favor owned-and-operated inventory since it costs them near-zero, unlike paying out to third parties like Disney+.
- **Nate Olmstead on guidance philosophy (same question, second part):** No change in approach; more data-driven than aspirational; explicitly reduced visibility disclosed.
- **Breaking down macro vs. execution vs. other factors, and long-term automation of the org (Jason Helfstein, Oppenheimer):** Green reiterated the weakness is concentrated in a handful of large, mostly CPG/Auto customers rather than systemic — did not provide a quantified split between macro and execution. Reiterated JBP growth (+38% YoY, 6x revenue growth rate), top-100 double-digit growth majority, CTV/audio double-digit growth, EMEA/APAC ~30% YTD growth, China >100% YTD growth as evidence the core business remains healthy. Did not directly answer the org-size/automation question beyond reiterating confidence in the new leadership team.

## Tone / Framing Notes

- Call opened with Jeff Green invoking the company's upcoming 10-year IPO anniversary and historical 34% revenue CAGR — used to contextualize this quarter as a blemish rather than a trend break.
- Green repeatedly stressed this is being framed internally as **cyclical, not systemic** — concentrated in a "handful" of large CPG/Auto accounts — while acknowledging the numbers don't yet prove that out.
- Explicit statement of increased capital discipline going forward: "some teams will continue to grow while others will not," investment tied to a strict "does it strengthen our ability to serve customers and drive long-term growth" test.
- No mechanism given yet for how/when a full-year outlook or updated long-term margin framework will be communicated — Olmstead deferred this to future updates.
