- **Number fact-check:**
  - Base EV **$171.3B**, or **$43.38**/TSLA share on 3.95B shares at $375.23.
  - Bear **−$33.5B**; Bull **$1,414.7B**; TSLA market cap ~**$1.48T**.
  - 6 driverless metros (Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa).
  - "8x in 30 days" is Tesla's own Oct 10 claim (300+ Cybercabs in Austin).
  - "Less than half the price of an Uber": Obi SF study, Tesla avg $8.17 vs Uber $17.47 (47%), late 2025. **Tesla had safety drivers there, and fares were likely subsidized.** Acknowledge this on the price slide (Slide 15).

  - Reverse DCF: **40.7M** cars needed for $1.48T, vs Bull 2036 avg fleet 10.5M (end-fleet 11.85M) ≈ **3.5–3.9x**. "Three and a half times" is the conservative wording.
  - Value split: operator $171B, software license ~$107B, car margin ~$22B. Supplier share = 129 / 300 ≈ **43%**.
- **"Most robotaxi valuations cheat"** is deliberately punchy. If it feels too strong, use "Most robotaxi valuations skip a step." The body of the video (Slide 24) shows the cost: inside-Tesla Base is $307.6B vs $171.3B standalone.
