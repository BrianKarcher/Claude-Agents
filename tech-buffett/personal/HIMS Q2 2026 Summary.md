# HIMS Q2 2026 Earnings Call Summary

Source: Hims & Hers Health Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Call transcript, 08-10-2026.

## Headline numbers
- Revenue: $753M, +40% YoY (domestic $622M, +16% YoY; international $131M, up ~17x YoY)
- Subscribers: ~3M globally, +300K net adds in the quarter
- Adjusted EBITDA: $60M (8% margin), excludes ~$81M of one-time costs (Eucalyptus deal costs, restructuring, FTC legal accrual)
- GAAP net loss: $86M
- Gross margin: 64%, down ~6 pts QoQ on mix shift to branded weight loss + international
- Cash & short-term investments: $840M+ (after ~$225M Eucalyptus payment); added a $400M receivables facility and $400M+ convertible debt
- Operating cash flow: -$36M in Q2; FCF -$68M; management expects FCF to resume in 2H

## Guidance
- Q3: revenue $880–900M (+47–50% YoY), adj. EBITDA $75–95M (~10% margin)
- FY2026: revenue raised to $3.1–3.3B (+32–41% YoY), adj. EBITDA $275–325M (~9% margin)
- Reaffirmed 2030 targets: $6.5B+ revenue, $1.3B adj. EBITDA

## Strategic themes
1. **AI as core infrastructure, not a bolt-on** — CTO Mo ElShenawy detailed a new AI-native care experience piloted on Hers Weight Loss (rolled out early July): 3x more customer messages, AI answering 80% of questions, ~50% reduction in non-clinical support tasks. Company is winding down third-party AI vendors, opening an AI R&D lab in Menlo Park, and expects AI investment payback in 12–18 months.
2. **Category expansion** — Testosterone (launched ~3 quarters ago) is scaling faster than any specialty besides weight loss and is on track to become the 6th U.S. specialty above a $100M run rate; injectable/oral TRT expansion planned by year-end. New Chief Medical Officer (Dr. Anant Vinjamoori) hired to lead hormonal health, longevity, and peptide therapy build-out.
3. **Peptides** — Awaiting final FDA rule-making on 6 peptides recommended at the July PCAC hearing before launching those; in the meantime plans to offer already-permitted wellness peptides (Sermorelin, Glutathione, etc.) by year-end. Management explicitly said it won't launch until *final* FDA rules are in place, not just proposed rule-making.
4. **International/Eucalyptus** — Closed the Eucalyptus acquisition in June (largest deal in company history), adding Australia/Japan reach and deepening Europe. Three international markets now above $100M run rate (UK, Australia, Germany); international expected to do $600M+ revenue in 2026, operating near breakeven as they prioritize scale. Eucalyptus itself running near breakeven/slight loss.
5. **Novo Nordisk partnership** — Called out as a strong and deepening relationship (oral Wegovy access), being extended into international markets via Eucalyptus's footprint.
6. **FTC litigation** — Briefly addressed: after ~3 years of cooperation and failed settlement talks, FTC filed a complaint July 29; company says it rejected terms it felt didn't reflect the facts/law and intends to "defend vigorously." This drove part of the $81M one-time charge.
7. **Long-term vision** — CEO described ambitions toward a "360 health" AI-driven membership/freemium platform (labs, wearables, real-time health scoring) explicitly compared to $50K–$150K/year concierge medicine, aiming to make that accessible broadly.

## Q&A takeaways
- Weight loss retention "in line with expectations," strong enough to drive the raised guide; management sees more room to improve via AI tools and lower prices later this year.
- Growth acceleration in 2H seen as driven mainly by monthly-cadence cohort stacking (branded weight loss) plus AI-driven efficiency reinvested into pricing/tools, not primarily new-therapy launches.
- Eucalyptus contributed only ~$4M of Q2 revenue (deal just closed in June); most of the guide raise is organic domestic acceleration.
- Q4 margin implied ~12% EBITDA at the midpoint per an analyst's math, which management didn't dispute but declined to use as a clean jump-off for FY27 guidance.
