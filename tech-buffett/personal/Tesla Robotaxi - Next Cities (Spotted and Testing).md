# Tesla Robotaxi — Next Cities (Spotted, Testing, or Staged)

_As of 2026-09-06. Cities where Tesla Robotaxi is NOT yet carrying public riders, but where
robotaxi-spec vehicles, staged fleets, or hiring point to an opening. Ordered by signal strength._

**How to read the signal tiers:**
- **Tier 1 — staged fleet / active road testing:** vehicles physically present in numbers.
  Launch typically follows within weeks to a few months.
- **Tier 2 — test vehicles spotted, no staged fleet:** could be an imminent market or just
  environment-hardening (weather, dense urban). Months out at least.
- **Tier 3 — hiring signal only:** "AI Safety Operator" / "Vehicle Operator" job posts, no
  confirmed vehicle sightings. Softest signal; Tesla posts broadly and ahead of itself.

Tell for a genuine robotaxi-spec car: the **rear + fender camera washers** ("Project Halo"
upfit), exclusive to network vehicles and not on consumer Model Ys. [2][4][7]

Bracketed numbers refer to the numbered **Sources** list at the bottom.

## Cybercab sightings (the purpose-built vehicle)

Separate from the Model Y fleet above. Cybercab presence is a stronger signal than a Model Y
sighting — it's a scarce, hand-allocated asset, so where Tesla sends it says a lot about launch
priority. As of early September 2026 the Texas DMV shows only ~45 Cybercabs registered, yet
staging lots hold far more, so most sightings are pre-deployment positioning.

| Location | What's been seen | Date | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Giga Texas, Austin, TX** (factory outbound lot) | ~60 units (Apr 8) → ~14 steering-wheel-less (Apr 17) → 100+ (June) → "hundreds" (July); some crash-tested | Apr–Jul 2026 | Production ramp, not a market signal — but confirms supply is running ahead of deployment. [13] |
| **Austin, TX** (public roads) | Street testing, then MoPac / Loop 1 **highway** testing, then **nighttime** highway testing; commercial launch with a small number of cars Sept 3 | through Sept 2026 | Already an operating market; shows the operational envelope widening (highway, night). [14] |
| **Dallas, TX** (robotaxi hub) | ~40 Cybercabs mid-June → ~100 by late August at a Dallas-area staging hub | Jun–Aug 2026 | Strongest non-Austin Cybercab concentration. Dallas already runs Model Y driverless — Cybercab public service there looks like the next step. [16] |
| **Miami, FL** (near MIA airport) | 20+ Cybercabs staged near Miami International Airport | ~Sept 2–3, 2026 | Miami runs Model Y driverless but the airport is currently outside the geofence — signals airport access + Cybercab both coming. [12] |
| **Philadelphia metro / Devon, PA** (showroom lot) | Multiple Cybercab test vehicles; fleet grew over a single weekend | ~Aug 22, 2026 | No Tesla Robotaxi geofence anywhere in Pennsylvania — one of the clearest out-of-market signals in the whole dataset. [12] |
| **Palo Alto, CA** (public roads) | Cybercab driven on public roads with no visible steering wheel | 2026 | Next to Tesla's Palo Alto engineering; likely R&D/validation rather than a market. CA has no Tesla AV approval. [15] |
| **San Francisco, CA** | Cybercab sightings began in the city | 2026 | Tesla's SF service still arrives with a driver at the wheel; no autonomous approval. Validation presence. [12] |
| **SunTrax, Central Florida** (closed test track) | Gov. DeSantis rode a Cybercab; "navigated all hazards" | 2026 | Private proving ground, not public roads — political/PR as much as technical. [12] |

One source also lists Cybercab **testing expanded to GA and NY** among "a handful of states,"
but with no specific city sightings — treat as unconfirmed. [16]

**Net:** the Cybercab data points to the same near-term order as the Model Y data (Dallas next
for Cybercab service, Miami airport, Austin envelope growth) plus one genuine surprise —
**Philadelphia**, which appears on no official list and has no Model Y geofence either.

## Watch-outs / caveats

- **California beyond the Bay Area** (LA, San Diego, Sacramento): Tesla has **not filed** for
  CPUC or DMV driverless permits. The Bay Area service runs under a limo permit with a safety
  driver. No LA/SD/Sacramento Tesla sightings. CA expansion is not close.
- **Don't confuse with Waymo:** Nashville, Atlanta, San Antonio, Denver, San Diego, Los Angeles
  are live or announced **Waymo** markets. Tesla's hiring touches some of those states but no
  Tesla robotaxi-spec cars have been documented there.
- **Job listings run ahead of reality** — Tesla posted operator roles in ~20 states while
  actually operating in 3. Weight staged fleets (Phoenix) far above job posts.
- **Geofence vs. new metro:** some "new city" sightings (Plano) are really coverage growth
  inside a market already open. Useful for mapping where existing metros thicken.

## Best current read on launch order

1. **Phoenix** (staged fleet, permissive state) — imminent
2. **Las Vegas** (permit clock running) — late 2026
3. **New Orleans** (fleet logging miles) — late 2026 / early 2027
4. **Denver/Aurora** (job posts, 7th state named) — 2027
5. **Chicago** (long-running test presence) — 2027, if the urban/winter validation clears
6. **Philadelphia** (Cybercab test fleet, no geofence, no official listing) — wildcard; watch it
7. Atlanta / Nashville / D.C. / NYC — 2027+, as #2 to Waymo
