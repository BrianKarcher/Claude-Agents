## MD&A — Revenue Drivers (management's own language, condensed)

Q2 revenue growth (+3% YoY) attributed to: more overall ad campaigns from new clients, partially offset by a decrease in gross spend from existing clients; a higher proportion of revenue earned from value-added services due to increased pricing; and a shift of certain supplier-provided component costs from being netted against revenue to being recorded as platform-operations expense. These increases were partially offset by volume/other discounts tied to Joint Business Plans and other strategic partnerships intended to drive future growth.

H1 revenue growth (+7% YoY) attributed to similar drivers plus more campaigns from both new and existing clients, and higher utilization of value-added services.

Company states it expects platform operations and technology & development opex to keep rising as it invests in hosting/platform infrastructure and AI, while aiming for "balanced" sales & marketing investment.

## Liquidity, Credit Facility & Legal Proceedings — Other Notes

- **Revolving Facility**: restated April 14, 2026 — now $750M capacity (up from $450M), $100M letter-of-credit sublimit, $75M swingline sublimit, matures April 14, 2031. Undrawn; $745M available (net of $5M in letters of credit) as of 6/30/2026. Company in compliance with all covenants.
- **Shelf registration (Form S-3)** on file, giving flexibility to raise capital via equity/equity-linked securities if needed — no indication of near-term use.
- **CFO signatory**: Nate Olmstead signed the 10-Q as Principal Financial Officer, confirming the CFO transition referenced in the Q1 extract's "permanent CFO" watchpoint is resolved as of this filing.

## One-Time Items / Accounting Notes

- **Change in useful life estimate**: effective April 1, 2026, useful life of certain data-center computing/networking equipment extended from 3 to 4 years. Reduced Q2 platform-ops depreciation by $4.6M, increased net income by $3.1M, added $0.01 to EPS. Expected to reduce FY2026 depreciation by ~$14M (from April 1 onward).
- G&A SBC continues to decline YoY — Q2 2025 included a $19M CEO Performance Option charge that had zero comparable charge in Q2 2026 (fully recognized as of Q1 2026).
- California SB 122 (enacted June 2026) extended annual limitations on R&D credit usage — no valuation allowance recorded yet, but a watch item (~$37M of CA R&D deferred tax assets at risk of delayed realizability).
- Effective tax rate remains elevated versus the 21% federal statutory rate, driven by non-deductible SBC and state/foreign taxes, partially offset by R&D credits.

## Legal Proceedings (Note 11 — status as of 6/30/2026 10-Q)

- **Nevada reincorporation litigation** (Gunderson / Scarantino actions, Delaware Chancery): ongoing; Delaware Supreme Court affirmed against the plaintiff on the books-and-records matter (6/26/2026); breach-of-fiduciary-duty claims still pending, no briefing schedule set.
- **Securities class action** ("In re The Trade Desk, Inc. Securities Litigation," C.D. Cal., covering purchases 11/15/2023–8/8/2025): motion to dismiss **denied** 3/17/2026; company answered 4/30/2026; trial date set for **May 16, 2028**. Management calls claims meritless, intends to defend.
- **Shareholder derivative actions** (multiple, CA/NV): largely stayed pending resolution of the securities class action.
- **Data privacy / wiretapping litigation** ("In re The Trade Desk, Inc. Data Privacy Litigation," N.D. Cal.): motion to dismiss mostly denied (12/18/2025); now in discovery, closing 9/10/2027.

No amounts accrued; management does not believe any of these are probable to have a material adverse effect, but litigation overhang (particularly the securities class action headed to a 2028 trial) remains a multi-year distraction.
