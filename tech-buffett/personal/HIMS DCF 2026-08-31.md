## Valuation Framework

**Approach:** 10-year explicit unlevered-FCF forecast (2026E–2035E). 2027E is discount year 1; the 2026E stub (a near-complete year; only ~$35M of slightly-negative FCF remained as of this model's date) is shown for continuity but **excluded** from the discounted sum. Two terminal-value methods, then averaged:
- **Method 1:** perpetuity growth (Gordon) on 2035E UFCF.
- **Method 2:** EV / adj. EBITDA exit multiple on 2035E adj. EBITDA.

**UFCF = (Adj. EBITDA − SBC − D&A) × (1 − tax) + D&A − CapEx − ΔNWC**
(SBC is treated as a real cash-equivalent cost — subtracted before tax; shares then held flat.)

**Discount rates:** Base **12.0%** — a profitable, fast-growing consumer-health platform with an equity-dominated capital structure (0% coupon converts), but carrying real execution risk: the branded-GLP-1 margin pivot, four international acquisitions integrating at once, structural gross-margin compression (82% → 64% over three years), FTC litigation, and a short track record of sustained GAAP profitability. Bull 10.5% (risks recede, platform compounds); Bear 13.5% (thesis strains).

## Key Value Drivers & Watch Items

**Supports the base case (and the path to $8B):**
1. **Guidance momentum.** FY2026 revenue guide raised twice in 2026 to $3.1–3.3B (+32–41%); Q3 guided +47–50% YoY. Growth is *re-accelerating*, mostly organically.
2. **ARPU compounding.** Monthly revenue per average subscriber $92 in Q2 2026, +21% YoY, as personalized/branded offerings and multi-specialty attach rise. On ~3M subscribers, every +$5/mo ≈ +$180M annual revenue.
3. **Category expansion.** Testosterone scaling faster than any specialty except weight loss (past $100M run-rate); hormonal health, longevity, labs, and FDA-permitted peptides layered on 2026–2028.
4. **International inflection.** Eucalyptus (Australia/Japan + deeper Europe) closed June 2026; three international markets already >$100M run-rate; international guided to $600M+ in 2026 at ~breakeven.
5. **AI leverage.** AI-native care experience cut non-clinical support tasks ~50% on the pilot; investment payback expected in 12–18 months; funds price cuts that drive volume.
6. **Marketing leverage.** 51% → 39% of revenue in three years; further leverage on a $4B+ base drops straight to EBITDA.

**Breaks the thesis (bear case):**
1. **Branded-GLP-1 economics.** If Novo/Lilly tighten distribution terms or pricing, or pull the partnership, the weight-loss specialty's margin and subscriber growth both deteriorate.
2. **FTC litigation.** Complaint filed July 29, 2026 after failed settlement talks; company will "defend vigorously." Overhang on sentiment and a potential cash/─remedy cost; partly drove the ~$81M one-time Q2 charge.
3. **Gross-margin compression proves structural.** 82% → 64% over three years; if it does not recover toward ~70%+, the 20% EBITDA target slips.
4. **Integration drag.** Four international acquisitions in ~18 months (Zava, Medici, LiveWell, Eucalyptus) across 8+ regulatory regimes; Eucalyptus itself running near breakeven/slight loss.
5. **Peptide regulation.** Company will not launch the 6 PCAC-recommended peptides until *final* FDA rules — timing uncertain; the peptide facility is a sunk cost until then.
6. **Convertible overhang / dilution.** ~$1.37B of converts; above ~$70.69 they add ~19–20M shares (partly hedged by capped calls).

## Assessment

**On the base-case premise:** if HIMS compounds revenue to $8.0B by 2030 (a ~25% 4-year CAGR, ~23% above the company's own target) and reaches its 20% EBITDA-margin goal on that larger base, intrinsic value is **~$58/share** — roughly double the current ~$29 price — with a method range of ~$46 (perpetuity) to ~$70 (11x exit). Under the perpetuity method alone, *every* cell of the WACC × terminal-growth grid sits above the current price.

**But the premise carries the argument.** $8B by 2030 is not management's guide; it is an above-plan outcome that requires branded GLP-1, peptides, international, and new specialties to all compound without a serious stumble, and the gross margin to recover ~7 points. The **bear case (~$18)** — where the GLP-1 economics or the FTC overhang break the wrong way and 2030 revenue lands near $5.7B — is ~38% *below* today's price. That asymmetry (down ~$11, up ~$29 to the base) is why the probability-weighted value (~$64) still sits well above the market: the market is currently pricing something close to the bear case.

**Rating: SPECULATIVE BUY on the base-case premise; position-size for a ~$18 downside.** The stock is priced for the thesis to fail. If Q3–Q4 2026 confirm the revenue re-acceleration and 2H FCF turns positive as guided, the risk/reward improves materially. Key confirmations to watch: (1) H2 2026 revenue actually hitting the implied ~$1.85B (Q3 $880–900M then a Q4 step-up); (2) adj. EBITDA margin reaching ~11–12% by Q4 2026; (3) FCF positive in 2H; (4) any FTC resolution or narrowing; (5) final FDA peptide rules.
